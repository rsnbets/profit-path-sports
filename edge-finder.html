⚡ What This Tool Does
Compare your available line to a sharper market line.
Drop in the odds you can bet (your softer book) and the odds you'd see at a sharp market (Pinnacle, Circa, an exchange). Edge Finder shows the probability gap between the two — the bigger the gap in your favor, the better your line is relative to where the market thinks the true price sits.
This is a fast line-shopping check, not true EV. For real EV, you need a fair (no-vig) probability — strip the vig with the Devig Calculator first, then plug it into the EV Calculator. The "Approx EV" stat above assumes the sharp line is the fair price, which is close but not exact (sharp books still bake in a small vig).
How to use this

Spot the gap between your line and the sharp market

  1. Enter the line you're being offered at a softer book (DraftKings, FanDuel, etc.).
  2. Enter the sharp market line for the same bet — Pinnacle, Circa, ProphetX, or whatever sharp source you have.
  3. Read the probability gap and approximate EV % — if positive, your softer book is offering a better number than the sharp consensus.
  4. Use as a fast +EV scan when you don't have time to devig the full market.
Why this version helps: When you're scrolling odds boards in real time, opening Devig + EV in two separate tabs slows you down. Edge Finder is a one-input shortcut — paste your line vs. the sharp line, get a quick read, decide whether to dig deeper.

Important caveat: the sharp line still has a small vig in it, so "Approx EV" overstates the true edge slightly. For precision before placing real money, devig the sharp market and run the result through the EV Calculator.
Edge Finder estimates value by comparing two lines — it's a quick line-shopping tool, not a true EV calculator.
