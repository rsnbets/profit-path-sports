How to Use a Bonus Bet on FanDuel

The single most common newbie mistake — placing a "free credit" bet without activating the credit, and accidentally betting your own real cash on a long shot. Here's the exact step you can't skip.

The critical step most newbies miss (60 seconds)

You've signed up at FanDuel, placed your $5 qualifying bet, and FanDuel has dropped $200 in free bet credits into your account. These are bonus bets — separate from your real cash balance. To use them, you have to explicitly tell FanDuel "use the bonus, not my cash" when you place each bet.

This is the #1 mistake total beginners make. They tap the bet they want, type in $10, hit Place Bet — and just spent $10 of their own real money on a long-shot bet that almost certainly loses. The free credits are still sitting in their account, unused.

The fix is one extra tap on the bet slip. Here's exactly where to find it.

Step-by-step

  1. Find your bonus bets first, before placing anything. They show up under Account → Promotions or at the top of any bet slip you open. The label is usually "Bonus Bets" or "Free Bets" with a dollar amount. If you don't see them, your qualifying bet might not have settled yet — wait a few hours.
  2. Find the bet you want to place. Pick your long-odds bet — usually a player prop ("Anytime Goal Scorer") or a big underdog moneyline. See How to Place a Bet on FanDuel if you need the navigation walkthrough.
  3. Tap the odds to add the bet to your bet slip. The slip slides up from the bottom (mobile) or appears on the right (desktop).
  4. Open the bet slip and look for the "Use Bonus Bet" option. On FanDuel this typically shows up as:
    • A toggle / switch labeled "Bonus Bets" or "Use Bonus Bet" near the stake field, OR
    • A dropdown menu where you can pick "Cash" or "Bonus Bet," OR
    • An "Apply Bonus Bet" button below the stake field
  5. Activate it. Tap the toggle / select the bonus from the dropdown. FanDuel will show you the available bonus credits — pick which one to apply (or pick a partial amount if FanDuel lets you split credits, which they currently do).
  6. Enter the amount of the bonus you want to use. For your first conversion, use $10 of your $200 pool (you can change this — FanDuel allows any amount up to your remaining credit). The slip will now show: "$10 stake (Bonus Bet)" instead of "$10 stake (Cash)."
  7. Verify the slip says "Bonus Bet" next to the stake. This is the critical check. If the slip just says "$10" without the word "Bonus" anywhere, you forgot to activate it — and you're about to bet real cash. Go back and activate.
  8. Place the bet. Your balance won't change (because it's bonus, not cash). The bet appears in your "Open Bets" section, marked as a bonus bet.

One thing to know — bonus bets pay differently

When a regular cash bet wins, you get your stake back PLUS the winnings. A $10 bet at +500 that wins pays you $60 total ($10 stake + $50 winnings).

When a bonus bet wins, you only get the winnings — not the stake. A $10 bonus bet at +500 that wins pays you $50 total (just the winnings, the $10 bonus is "consumed").

This is why bonus conversion works on long-odds bets: at +500 odds, you're betting bonus credit to try to win $50 in real cash. If you place the matching hedge bet on ProphetX correctly, you turn that gamble into a guaranteed ~$8 cash regardless of outcome.

Common gotchas

  • You forgot to activate the bonus. Slip says "$10" but no "Bonus Bet" label anywhere. You're about to bet cash. Always check before tapping Place Bet.
  • You used too much of the bonus on one bet. If you wanted to use $10 but accidentally typed $25, FanDuel will use $25 of your bonus pool. This isn't a disaster — it just means a bigger hedge on ProphetX. Re-run the Bonus Converter with the actual amounts.
  • The bonus didn't show up yet. FanDuel can take a few minutes to a few hours after your qualifying bet settles. If it's been more than 24 hours and your qualifier won/lost, contact FanDuel support — they'll find it.
  • Bonus bet has an odds restriction. Some welcome offers say the bonus must be used on bets with odds longer than a certain minimum (e.g., "-200 or higher"). FanDuel will usually flag this on the slip if your pick doesn't qualify. Check the welcome offer terms once.
  • You can't combine bonus and cash on one bet. A single bet is either 100% bonus or 100% cash — no mixing. If you want to split a $25 bonus across multiple bets, place them as separate slips.
