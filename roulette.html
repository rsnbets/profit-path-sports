Virtual Roulette
⚡ What This Tool Does
A built-in house edge means you can't out-play roulette.
Place real bets on a virtual table — single spin, or run the same bet across thousands of spins to see what the math does to your bankroll. Toggle between the American (0/00) wheel and the European (single 0) wheel to see how a tiny rule difference compounds into a very different outcome.
Roulette pays out as if there were no zeros — but the zeros exist. That gap is the house edge: 5.26% on American, 2.70% on European. Over enough spins, your expected loss equals your total wagered times that edge — no system, betting pattern, or "due" number changes that.
⚡ Why It Matters
Sports betting at fair prices can be +EV. Casino games can't. This tool shows you the difference, in dollars, on a simulated bankroll.
⚡ House Edge
Every payout is one unit short of fair. American (5.26%) is nearly 2× as costly as European (2.70%) per spin.
⚡ Read the Stats
Compare your actual loss to "expected loss" (wagered × edge). At low spin counts, variance dominates. At high counts, the edge wins.
