The Sharp Exchange Playbook

The most underexplored edge in modern sports betting. Read this curriculum top to bottom and you'll find +EV bets the average bettor doesn't even know exist.

Exchange-based betting (ProphetX, Novig) is where trading firms, syndicates, and serious bettors set prices. The order books on those platforms tell you what the sharp money is doing in real time — if you know how to read them. This is the playbook for that skill. Five short lessons plus one dedicated calculator. Work through them in order, then start scanning. Nothing else on the internet covers this for free.

What you'll have when you're done

  • The translation rule — how to read every offered price as the inverse of the sharp's actual position
  • The BEST-vs-PX heuristic for spotting cross-market EV bets in seconds
  • How to identify outsized liquidity on thin markets and read it as concentrated sharp conviction
  • The conviction overflow signal — exchange depth exceeding book limits = leading indicator of line movement
  • The maker tactic — when to post your own price instead of taking the existing best
  • A working calculator (Sharp Snapshot) that automates all of the above on any market
