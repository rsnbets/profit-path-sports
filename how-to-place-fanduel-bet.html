How to Place a Bet on FanDuel

Never placed a sportsbook bet before? Here's the 4-tap process: pick a game, tap the odds, enter your amount, confirm. Plus what to look for on the bet slip and common mistakes.

What you're about to do (60 seconds)

Placing a bet on FanDuel is a 4-tap process: pick a game, tap an odds button, enter how much you want to bet, confirm. The interface looks more complex than it is because FanDuel shows you thousands of betting options across hundreds of games — but the actual bet-placement flow is the same for every bet.

This walkthrough covers a standard cash bet. If you want to apply a free bet credit (from a welcome offer), see How to Use a Bonus Bet on FanDuel for the one-extra-step version.

Step-by-step

  1. Open the FanDuel Sportsbook app or website and log in. You should land on a home screen with a list of sports across the top (NFL, NBA, MLB, etc.) and featured games below.
  2. Find the game you want to bet on. Tap a sport from the top bar (e.g., MLB). You'll see a list of games happening today and upcoming. Tap the specific game (e.g., "Tigers @ Yankees").
  3. Find the bet you want to place. Each game page shows several betting markets — Moneyline (who wins), Spread (margin of victory), Total (combined score over/under), and Player Props (individual player stats). Tap any of these to see the specific options. You're looking for a button showing the odds (e.g., "+500" or "-110") next to your pick.
  4. Tap the odds. The bet gets added to your bet slip — a small panel that slides up from the bottom or sits on the right side of the screen. The slip shows the bet you just selected with a place to enter your stake.
  5. Open the bet slip and enter your wager amount. Type the dollar amount you want to bet (e.g., $5 for the qualifying bet). The slip will show you the "To Win" amount automatically — that's your potential winnings if the bet wins.
  6. Tap "Place Bet" at the bottom of the slip. FanDuel will confirm the bet is placed and show you a confirmation screen. Your account balance drops by the stake amount.

Reading the bet slip

Before you tap Place Bet, your slip should clearly show:

  • The bet description — e.g., "Tigers Moneyline" or "Patrick Mahomes Anytime TD Scorer"
  • The odds — e.g., +500 (means a $1 bet wins you $5)
  • Your stake — how much you're betting
  • "To Win" — your profit if the bet wins (not including the return of your stake)
  • "Total Payout" — your stake + winnings if it hits (this is what gets added to your balance)

If anything on the slip looks wrong, tap the X to remove the bet and try again.

Common gotchas

  • You're not physically in a legal state. FanDuel uses your phone's GPS to verify your location. If you're outside a legal sports betting state, the bet will be blocked. Turn off any VPN.
  • Your balance is below the bet amount. If you try to bet $10 with only $5 in your account, FanDuel will reject the bet. Deposit more or lower the stake.
  • Odds changed between adding to slip and placing. Lines move fast, especially close to game time. If the odds changed, FanDuel will show you the new price and ask you to confirm. Always glance at the final number before tapping Place Bet.
  • Same Game Parlay (SGP) buttons are different. If you're tapping multiple bets in the same game, FanDuel might automatically build them into a parlay. For a standard single bet, only tap one button per slip. (Single bets are what you want for bonus conversion — never parlays.)
  • You accidentally tapped a long-shot prop. If your bet slip shows +1500 odds when you meant -110, you tapped the wrong button. Clear the slip and try again.
