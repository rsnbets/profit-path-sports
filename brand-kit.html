Color system

Six core colors. The rest are tints, surfaces, and text alphas derived from them. Cyan is the everyday accent (labels, links, routine UI); green is reserved for things that really matter (lock-this-in moments, key warnings, positive outcomes). Don't use green casually — it loses its weight.

02 · Typography

Type stack

Two fonts. Space Grotesk for display (titles, numbers, eyebrows, anything that needs presence). Plus Jakarta Sans for body and UI text — lighter default weight (300) for editorial feel, with bolder weights for emphasis.

Every bet you see listed on an exchange has been created by one or more other users. It doesn't exist until somebody writes it.
