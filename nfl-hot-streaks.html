How to use the NFL streak scanner

  1. Pick the prop — anytime TD, 50+ rushing, 4+ receptions, whatever you'd actually bet.
  2. Read the streak. Dots are his last 10 games: green cashed, red missed. A carried tag means part of the run dates to last season — real, but a different team context now.
  3. Yardage streaks only count usage games. A receiver's zero-carry week doesn't break (or pad) a rushing streak — games without that kind of usage are excluded on purpose.
  4. Rank by Last 3 / 5 / 10 to catch the guy who's 4-of-5 but missed once and lost his "streak."
  5. Tap a player to open his full prop page — clear rates at any line, game chart, home/away splits.
Straight talk: football is a 17-game sample sport. Every streak here is short by baseball standards, defenses change weekly, and the market knows the same run you do. Use the streak to find the player — the price (coming soon) makes the bet.
