How to read a board

Every board gives each team a share of the break's value, then turns that into a fair price and a buy below price. Pay at or under the green number. Over the fair number you're paying the breaker's margin plus a premium.

Share comes from the checklist

Not from team popularity. A team with six base cards and no premium insert slots is worth less than a rebuilding team with one star on every 1:600 insert — no matter how the room feels about them.

Set the breaker's total

Add up every spot the breaker is selling and type that number in. The whole board rescales to it, so the targets match the break in front of you rather than a hypothetical one.

Boards are a read of each release's checklist plus current prospect hype at the time of writing. They're a pricing guide, not a guarantee — card values move. New boards get added here as checklists drop.
