How to use this

What it does. You see a row on Pick the Odds with prices and dollar amounts across Pinnacle, Circa, ProphetX, and Novig. Type those numbers into the cards above. The tool runs the analysis we walk through manually in our guides — translation rule on the exchanges, devig on Pinnacle, consensus fair, and the target price you need to beat at a soft book.

Inputs. Use American odds (e.g. −110, +135). For the dollar amounts: Pinnacle and Circa are book limits (the max they'll book at that line); ProphetX and Novig are exchange order-book depth (real user-posted money). Leave any source blank if Pick the Odds doesn't show it for that market.

The four outputs:

  • Sharp Translation — translates exchange depth to the sharp's actual position (flip side, flip sign). Tallies how much sharp capital sits on each side and shows the imbalance ratio.
  • Conviction Check — compares exchange depth to book limits on each side. When exchange depth exceeds book limits, sharps wanted more capacity than the books would underwrite — they overflowed onto the exchange. That's a strong conviction signal and a leading indicator of line movement.
  • Consensus Fair — weighted no-vig fair price across the available sources. Pinnacle and Novig get the heaviest weight since they're the deepest references.
  • Soft-Book Target — the price you need to beat at a soft book to capture cross-market EV. The "primary" side is whichever the sharps want (heavier imbalance); the "secondary" side is the other one, lower-edge by default.

Reading the verdict:

  • Strong (5×+ imbalance) — sharps clearly on one side, big confidence to act on it.
  • Solid (2×-5×) — meaningful directional signal.
  • Mild (1.3×-2×) — small lean, treat with caution.
  • Flat (below 1.3×) — no clear sharp signal, just take the consensus fair.

Caveats.

  • Book limits (Pinnacle, Circa) are confidence indicators, not sharp positions. The translation tally uses ONLY exchange depth.
  • For very thin markets ($100-$300 depth), single rec bettors can mimic sharp signals. Larger absolute depths give more confident reads.
  • Once you have the target price, the cross-market EV play is to find it at a soft book — see Cross-Market EV for the full strategy.
