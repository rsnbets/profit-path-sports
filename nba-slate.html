How to read the slate

  1. Pick the game, then the market. Points, rebounds, assists, threes, combos, defense, turnovers, minutes: the same tabs you would walk at a props site. "All games" shows the whole date.
  2. Line = the market's main number on that player right now, and every hit rate is graded against that number, not a round one. Best is the top price for the side you're on; Fair is the vig-free price from the two-sided books. A line in blue means no book has posted one yet, so it is his own number: the median of his last 20 games played, on a half.
  3. L5 / L10 / L20 / Season are how often he has cleared it (or stayed under, if you flip the side). Last is his most recent game. Rows sort by L10, so the streaks float up. Click any column to sort.
  4. Matchup is what tonight's opponent allows per game to his position (guards, forwards, centers), ranked 1 to 30 (1 = stingiest; green = soft). Under five games this season it uses last season's final ranks. Report is ESPN's current injury designation.
  5. + Playoffs / Reg season switches postseason games in or out of every window and of his number.
🔒 Lock this in A hit rate is a description, not a prediction. The price is what you pay for it: a 9/10 at −160 and a 6/10 at +120 can be the same bet. Open the player's page to test the exact number your book hangs.
