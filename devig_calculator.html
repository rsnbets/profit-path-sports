How to use this

Devig any market in 30 seconds

  1. Find a market on any sportsbook with both sides priced (e.g. Lakers −110 / Mavs +100).
  2. Plug both prices into the input boxes — American (−175), decimal (1.57), or implied probability (63.64%) all work.
  3. Read the Fair No-Vig column on the right. Those are the true probabilities and odds the line implies once the book's vig is removed.
  4. Compare the no-vig odds to what's available at other sportsbooks. If you can get a better price than the no-vig line at another book, that bet is +EV.
Total Implied shows the sum of both raw prices — anything over 100% is the vig the book is charging on this market. Hold % is what the book keeps over time at that vig. The lower the hold, the sharper the market.
