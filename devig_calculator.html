Method
Proportional, Additive, Power — which method?

All three strip the bookmaker's vig out of a two-way market — they just split it differently.

Proportional (default) removes the vig in proportion to each side's price. It's the industry-standard method and what most sharp tools use. Start here.

Additive removes an equal slice of vig from each side. It hands underdogs slightly more fair probability than Proportional does.

Power solves for an exponent that pulls both sides down until they sum to exactly 100%. It's widely considered the most accurate on lopsided markets — heavy favorites and longshots — and the method many sharp bettors prefer there.

On balanced markets (around −110 / −110) all three land within a fraction of a percent of each other. They diverge on lopsided lines. If switching methods flips your read, treat the edge as fragile and lean toward passing.

What is devigging, in plain English?

Every price at a sportsbook has a hidden fee built in — the vig — and it makes each side's implied win chance read higher than the truth. That's why both sides of a market add up to more than 100%. This tool strips the fee out so you can see the true odds underneath.

How to read the outputs: Fair No-Vig Price is the real odds and win chance for each side. In Market Analysis, Total Implied is the two raw prices added up (anything over 100% is the fee), Market Vig is that overround, and Hold % is what the book keeps long-run — lower hold means a sharper market.

Which book should you devig? (Garbage in, garbage out)

A no-vig price is only as sharp as the market you pulled it from. Devig a soft retail book — BetMGM, Bet365 — and the "fair" line just tells you what that book thinks, which usually isn't sharp at all. For a fair price worth trusting, devig the sharpest market you can find: Pinnacle, Circa, or a sharp consensus line.

What do I do with the fair price?

Compare it to what other books are offering. If a book lets you bet a side at a better price than its no-vig fair odds, that bet is +EV — you're being paid more than the true chance justifies. Want the whole pipeline — devig, EV, Kelly sizing, and a verdict — in one shot? That's Bet X-Ray.
