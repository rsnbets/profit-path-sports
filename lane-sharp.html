Why This Still Matters For You

You know the math. So why bother with another free toolkit?

Because every minute you save plugging numbers into a clean tool is a minute back hunting edges. The calculators here are the same math you already run — devig, EV, Kelly, hedge — just packaged so you can paste a line into a clean tab and get the answer in two seconds. No signup. No log-in flow. No "premium tier" gating the actually-useful features.

The Bet Tracker stays in your browser. The Long Run Simulator runs Monte Carlos client-side so your edge math doesn't leave your device. The Parlay Calculator surfaces compounded vig honestly so you can talk yourself out of bad parlays as fast as you confirm the rare good ones. None of that is novel — it's just the basics done right and free.

And if a recreational friend ever asks how you actually do it, sending them to one page beats explaining a spreadsheet they wouldn't open anyway. The lane structure on the homepage gives them a path; you get to keep using the tools you already needed.
