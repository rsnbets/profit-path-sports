What you'll likely get

BetMGM's welcome offer is a first-bet match — fundamentally different from the "Bet $5, get $X" structure at DraftKings and FanDuel. The current offer (Colorado; varies by state):

Make your first bet at any amount up to $1,500. If it loses, BetMGM refunds your stake in bonus bets matching what you wagered (capped at $1,500). If it wins, no bonus — you keep the cash.

Exact terms can vary by state and rotate occasionally — always read the fine print on the actual signup page before depositing.

The key distinction:

  • This is not a free bonus regardless of outcome — it's a loss-protected first bet
  • The bonus only triggers if your first bet loses
  • The refund comes as bonus bets, not cash (same conversion rules as DK/FD: ~70% to real money)
  • To maximize the offer, you want your first bet to lose more often than it wins — which means betting a long-odds underdog
  • To minimize variance, you hedge that underdog on a second sportsbook so you collect either way

This is why BetMGM is the last book in the Path 02 ladder: you need ~$1,500 in deployable capital for the qualifier, plus enough at a second book to fund the hedge. You don't have that capital on day one — you build it through the first five books, then deploy it here.

How to convert it

First-bet match offers convert best with a long-odds qualifier + opposing hedge strategy. The logic: long shots usually lose, which triggers the bonus refund. Hedging at a second book locks in roughly similar cash whether the qualifier wins or loses. Quick walkthrough:

  1. Find a long-odds bet at BetMGM — ideally +400 to +600. Could be a futures pick, a long-shot moneyline, a high-line player prop. See How to find bonus conversion plays for three ways to surface candidates fast (OddsJam Promo Converter, PTO Premium arb finder, or manual scan).
  2. Decide your qualifier stake — up to $1,500. Most people max it out to get the full match value
  3. Use the Bonus Bet Converter + your hedge math to figure out the hedge stake at a second sportsbook (e.g. FanDuel on the other side)
  4. Place the qualifier at BetMGM and the hedge at the second book at the same time
  5. If the qualifier loses (most likely outcome at +400): your hedge wins, you get $1,500 in bonus bets back. Convert each bonus bet using the long-odds + hedge approach. Net cash: ~$1,050 after conversion
  6. If the qualifier wins (less likely): you cash a much bigger payout on the long shot, lose the hedge stake. Net cash: similar range, sometimes higher
  7. Either path → ~$700-$1,050 in real, withdrawable cash for the full deployment
