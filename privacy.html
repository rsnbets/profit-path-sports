Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026

Profit Path Sports ("we," "us," or "the site") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and what we do with it. Plain English, no dark patterns.

The short version

We don't sell, rent, or share your data. We don't use behavioral ad tracking. The only personal data we collect is your email address — and only if you voluntarily send it to us to request the Sharp Exchange Playbook or to ask a question. You can opt out or request deletion at any time.

What we collect

Information you give us directly

The only personal information we collect is information you choose to send us:

  • Email address — if you email info@profitpathsports.com to request the Sharp Exchange Playbook, ask a question, or send feedback.
  • The content of your message — whatever you write in your email.

Information collected automatically

This site is hosted on Vercel, which automatically logs standard web-server data for every visit:

  • IP address (partially anonymized in Vercel's standard logs)
  • Browser type, operating system, and device type
  • Pages visited, referring URL, timestamp

We do not use this data to identify individual visitors. It exists primarily for security, abuse prevention, and aggregate analytics (e.g. how many people visited the homepage last week).

Information we do NOT collect

  • Behavioral advertising cookies or trackers
  • Social-media tracking pixels
  • Cross-site tracking identifiers
  • Your name, location, or financial information (unless you voluntarily send it via email)
  • Browser fingerprinting

How we use your information

If you email us, we use your email address for one purpose: to reply to you. If you requested the Sharp Exchange Playbook, we'll send you the link. If you asked a question, we'll answer it. If you sent feedback, we'll read it.

We may use your email address to occasionally notify you about new content if you've explicitly opted in (e.g. by subscribing to a newsletter). We currently do not run a formal newsletter — when we do, opt-in will be explicit and you'll be able to unsubscribe at any time.

Server-log data (IP, browser, etc.) is used to identify abuse patterns, debug technical issues, and understand aggregate traffic. It is not combined with your email or used to build a profile of you.

What we don't do with your information

  • We don't sell your data. Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to anyone.
  • We don't share your email address with any third party, except as required by law.
  • We don't use your email to send unsolicited marketing.
  • We don't profile you for ad targeting.

Third-party services

This site uses the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy that governs how they handle data they receive from your visit:

  • Vercel — hosting and content delivery. Vercel Privacy Policy ↗
  • Google Fonts — webfont delivery. Google receives your IP when fonts load. Google Privacy Policy ↗

External links from this site (e.g. to sportsbooks, betting exchanges, third-party tools) take you to sites we don't control. Once you click those links, those sites' privacy policies apply, not ours.

Affiliate links

Some links on this site are affiliate links — when you click them and complete a qualifying action (typically signing up and depositing at a sportsbook), we may receive a referral commission. Those links typically pass a tracking code to the destination site that identifies us as the referrer, but they do not transmit your personal information to us. See our affiliate disclosure page for the full list and details.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Know what data we have about you (in practice: your email address, if you've emailed us, plus whatever's in your messages).
  • Request a copy of any personal information we hold about you.
  • Request deletion of your email and message history. We honor these requests within 30 days, and typically within 24-48 hours.
  • Opt out of any future communications from us at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email info@profitpathsports.com with "Privacy request" in the subject line.

Children

This site is intended for adults of legal sports-betting age (21+ in most U.S. jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has sent us information, please email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy over time as the site evolves (for example, if we add a newsletter signup form or analytics tooling). When we do, we'll change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Substantial changes will be noted clearly. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Questions or requests?

Email us at info@profitpathsports.com. We try to respond to privacy-related messages within 48 hours.
