Step 3 · Your Picks — Sharp Market Odds Per Leg
How to use

Reading the checker

  1. Pick your platform and slip type. That sets the benchmark you have to beat on each leg, and the payout multiplier when all legs hit.
  2. Custom payout (optional). If your ticket has goblins / demons / a boost — or you're on DK Pick6 where slips often show "at least 5.5x" — flip on Custom payout and type the exact multiplier shown on the ticket. The implied-odds-per-pick benchmark recalculates from mult^(–1/picks) automatically.
  3. Choose your mode. Devig fair odds is the right input — the no-vig consensus price from a sharp book. If you only have the best book's vigged odds, use Best book odds mode (the slip EV will be conservative — your actual edge is bigger after devigging).
  4. Enter American odds for each leg. Each row shows the leg's implied probability, the edge vs. the slip benchmark, and a per-leg verdict (BEATS / MISSES).
  5. Read the top bar. The Slip Verdict banner tells you if the entire slip is +EV at the prices you entered. The five stats show: probability all legs hit, the break-even hit rate you need to clear, the slip's payout multiplier, the EV per $100 entry, and the ROI.
The core idea: any slip type can be +EV if every leg beats that slip's implied odds per pick. The "best" slip isn't inherently better — it just has a lower benchmark (e.g., -116 on a 6-man vs. -200 on a 2-pick). Your job: find picks with sharper market lines than the bar your slip sets.
