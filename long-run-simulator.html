⚡ What This Tool Does
See what your edge actually looks like over thousands of bets.
Even a real, profitable edge doesn't grow your bankroll in a straight line — variance can swing you up and down for weeks before the math catches up. This simulator runs your edge through a Monte Carlo simulation: it plays out hundreds of possible bettor "lifetimes" using your inputs, then shows you the full range of outcomes side-by-side.
Plug in your edge, your average odds, how many bets you place, and your Kelly fraction. Each simulated path bets day-by-day, sizing each bet from the current bankroll. The chart shows every path; the bold yellow line is the median. The stats bar tells you the realistic best, worst, and middle outcomes — plus the chance you go bust along the way.
⚡ Why It Matters
A "+EV" bettor can still have a losing month. Seeing the spread of paths sets realistic expectations and saves your bankroll.
⚡ Why Kelly Fraction
Full Kelly maximizes growth but swings hard. Fractional Kelly (½ or ¼) sacrifices some upside for much smaller drawdowns.
