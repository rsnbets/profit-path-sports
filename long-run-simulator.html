Long Run Simulator

Even a real +EV edge produces brutal drawdowns. Most bettors quit during the variance, not when they actually lose the edge. Enter your edge and bet count — see the realistic best, worst, and median bankroll paths via Monte Carlo simulation, all client-side.

How to use this ↓
⚡ What This Tool Does
See what your edge actually looks like over thousands of bets.
Even a real, profitable edge doesn't grow your bankroll in a straight line — variance can swing you up and down for weeks before the math catches up. This simulator runs your edge through a Monte Carlo simulation: it plays out hundreds of possible bettor "lifetimes" using your inputs, then shows you the full range of outcomes side-by-side.
Plug in your edge, your average odds, how many bets you place, and your Kelly fraction. Each simulated path bets day-by-day, sizing each bet from the current bankroll. The chart shows every path; the bold yellow line is the median. The stats bar tells you the realistic best, worst, and middle outcomes — plus the chance you go bust along the way.
⚡ Why It Matters
A "+EV" bettor can still have a losing month. Seeing the spread of paths sets realistic expectations and saves your bankroll.
⚡ Why Kelly Fraction
Full Kelly maximizes growth but swings hard. Fractional Kelly (½ or ¼) sacrifices some upside for much smaller drawdowns.

How to use this

Run your edge through 1,000+ bets

  1. Enter your edge per bet — e.g. 2% if you think your average bet is +2% EV. (If you don't know your real edge, use 1-3% as a realistic starting range.)
  2. Enter your stake size and the total number of bets you want to simulate.
  3. Pick a Kelly fraction (or set a flat-stake mode) to match how you actually bet.
  4. Read the best / median / worst bankroll outcomes from the Monte Carlo runs and the equity curve below.
Why this version helps: Most edge simulators show a single curve. We run thousands of paths and show the full distribution of outcomes — best case, worst case, median — because variance is brutal and the median path you'd actually walk often looks nothing like the headline expected value.

What you'll see: a bettor with a real 3% edge can still be down 10+ units after 200 bets. The long run rewards patience and proper bankroll management — this tool is the proof.
Monte Carlo simulations show possible outcomes — not predictions. Real-world variance, bankroll caps, and book limits will differ.
