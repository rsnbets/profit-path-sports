How to use this

Find guaranteed-profit arbs

  1. Enter the odds for both sides — must be from different sportsbooks for a real arb to exist.
  2. Enter your total stake budget across the two bets.
  3. Calculator splits the stake to lock in equal profit either way and shows profit % + profit $.
  4. If profit is positive, you have an arb. Place both sides quickly before the lines move.
Why this version helps: Most arb calculators return weird stakes like $34.27 / $65.73 — fine on paper, awkward at the cashier window. We show both the exact mathematically optimal split and a rounded version (whole dollars) so you can place real-world bets without doing the rounding yourself.

One thing to know: books detect repeat arbing — vary your stake sizes, don't always pick the sharpest angle, and pass on small edges to stay under the radar.
