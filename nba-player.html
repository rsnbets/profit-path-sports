How to use the NBA prop finder

  1. Search a player, pick a prop. Step the line to the number your book hangs. Every panel recomputes against it.
  2. Read the matchup and the designation. The matchup card is what tonight's opponent allows per game to his position (guards, forwards or centers), ranked against all 30. Green = soft. The badge on his name is ESPN's current injury designation.
  3. Read the clear rates. L20/L10/L5 show form. "vs" tonight's opponent shows the head-to-head with the sample printed on it, because two meetings is trivia, not a trend.
  4. Choose your sample. "+ Playoffs" keeps postseason games in every window. "Reg season" drops them. Playoff minutes and rotations are a different sport, so check both before you trust a rate.
  5. Flip Over/Under. Every panel follows the side you are actually betting.
  6. Build the combo before you take an SGP. Pick two or more of his props and the panel counts the games where all of them landed. Two props on one player move together, so the real chance is usually higher than multiplying the prices.
  7. Mind the season boundary. Until this season's games pile up, most of what you read is last season: same player, maybe a new team, a new coach, a new role.
