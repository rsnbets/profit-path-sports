What you'll likely get

Hard Rock's welcome offer is the win-trigger variant — fundamentally different from DK's and FD's "always-bonus" structures. The current offer:

Bet $5 — if it wins, get $150 in bonus bets. If your qualifier loses, no bonus.

Exact promo terms can vary by state and rotate occasionally — odds minimums on the qualifier may apply (always read the fine print on the actual signup page before depositing).

Why this structure flips the play:

  • With Bet & Get (DK, FD): the bonus comes regardless, so you bet the safest tiny qualifier you can find — minimize loss on the $5
  • With Win-Trigger (Hard Rock): the bonus only triggers if you win, so you bet the most-likely-to-win qualifier — a heavy favorite at deep odds-on (-300, -400, -500, whatever the line offers)
  • Heavy favorites win the majority of the time → you trigger the bonus the majority of the time
  • You then hedge the underdog at a second book to limit downside if the favorite happens to lose
  • Net: locked-in cash either way, with the bonus-trigger case (most common) being the big payout

The bonus itself works the same as DK / FD: $150 in non-withdrawable bonus bets, convertible to ~$105 in real cash via the standard long-odds-plus-hedge play (see Bonus Conversion 101 for the mechanic).

How to convert it

Win-trigger offers convert best with the heavy-favorite-plus-hedge play. The logic: a heavy favorite at deep odds-on wins the vast majority of the time → triggers the bonus → which you then convert through the normal long-odds + hedge mechanic. Quick walkthrough:

  1. Find a heavy favorite at Hard Rock — typically a moneyline at -300 or shorter (e.g. heavy-favorite NBA / NFL line, or a soccer moneyline on a clear mismatch). Implied probability ≈ 75%+ win rate. Check Hard Rock's terms for any minimum-odds requirement on the qualifier.
  2. Place a $5 qualifier on that favorite at Hard Rock
  3. Hedge the underdog at a second sportsbook using your existing accounts (DK, FD, ProphetX, whatever). Sized so that if the favorite loses, the hedge profit roughly offsets the missed bonus. Use the Hedge Calculator to size the hedge precisely.
  4. Most likely outcome (~75-85%): favorite wins → you collect a small profit on the qualifier, lose the hedge, and $150 in bonus bets lands in your Hard Rock account. Convert those bonus bets via the standard long-odds + hedge play — see How to find bonus conversion plays for the three ways to surface long-odds candidates fast — for ~$105 cash. Total: ~$80-90 net cash for the play.
  5. Less likely outcome (~15-25%): favorite loses → no bonus, but your hedge at the second book wins, offsetting the qualifier loss. Net cash close to zero — you broke even instead of losing.
