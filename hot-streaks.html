How to use the streak scanner

  1. Pick the prop you're interested in — Over 0.5 hits, Over 1.5 total bases, pitcher Ks, whatever you'd actually bet.
  2. Read the streak — that's how many straight games (or starts) the player has cashed that exact bet. The dots show the last 15: green cashed, red missed.
  3. Switch the ranking if you want recency over perfection — Last 5 / 10 / 15 ranks by most cashes in that window, which catches the guy who's 9-of-10 but missed once and lost his "streak."
  4. Check the season rate next to it. A 9-game run from a guy who clears the bar 70% of the season is a very different animal than a 9-game run from a 45% guy.
  5. Look at the price. Best Over is the top live price across every book we track. Fair Odds is the vig-free market consensus — what the bet is "really worth" tonight.
  6. Tap a row to see every line the books have posted for him tonight — tap any of them to compare the Over price across all books and shop the number before you bet.
  7. "No O0.5 line" means exactly that. Some players only get an alt line hung (a 2+ hits price with no 1+ price). This board won't quote you that number next to a 1+ streak, because it's a different bet — open the row and the alt lines are there, clearly labelled.
Straight talk: a streak is not an edge. Books see the same run you do, and the odds already price it in — sometimes with a "hot player tax" on top. Decades of research on the hot hand say the next game looks a lot more like the season rate than the streak.
🔒 Lock this in The streak finds the player — the price makes the bet. Use this page as a discovery tool, then compare Best Over against Fair Odds. If the best price beats fair, you're getting a good number. If it's well below fair, you're paying the hot-player tax — pass, no matter how pretty the dots look.
