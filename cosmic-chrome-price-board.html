Premium inserts

Planetary Pursuit, Mars Attacks, Anti-Gravity, Constellation Variation, Cosmic Dust, Planetarium. These are 1:600+ pulls and carry most of the insert money. Base cards are a dollar each. This column is where the value is.

Autos: six sets, not one

Cosmic Chrome Autos fall 1:136 packs, but five more autograph sets sit behind them — Extraterrestrial Talent, Launched Into Orbit, Stellar Stars, Star Clusters Duals and Cut Signatures — plus their parallels. Together that is about one auto per 2.7 boxes, so a full case averages three. Any single box still comes up empty about 7 times in 10, which is why auto count nudges a spot rather than setting its price.

Breaker total

Add up every spot on the board. $5,500 assumes a breaker paid about $4,400 for a case and takes 25%. If their spots add up to more, type that number in. For auctioned randoms, ignore this and bid against card value: under $65 a spot on a case is value, over $200 is overpaying.

Share is a read of the checklist plus current prospect hype as of early October 2026. If Murakami, Griffin or McGonigle has a big spring, their teams move up.
