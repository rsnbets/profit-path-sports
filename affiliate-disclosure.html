We disclose every affiliate relationship we have. Affiliate compensation does not influence which products we recommend or how we describe them.

What an affiliate link is

An affiliate link (also called a referral link or partner link) is a regular link with a tracking parameter that tells the destination site we sent you. If you sign up and meet certain conditions (typically depositing money and/or placing a qualifying first bet), the destination site pays us a commission for the referral. It costs you nothing extra. The price, offers, and terms you receive from the sportsbook are identical whether you arrive through our link or not.

This is the same business model used by virtually every sports-betting media site, comparison site, and review aggregator. The FTC requires sites that earn referral commissions to disclose those relationships clearly — this page is our disclosure.

How to spot affiliate links on our site

All affiliate links on Profit Path Sports use HTML attributes that flag the link as sponsored: rel="nofollow noopener sponsored". In practice, you can also assume any link to a sportsbook signup page, a referral-code signup form, or a paid tool's signup page is affiliate. We're not hiding it.

Our editorial independence

What affiliate compensation does NOT change

  • We don't promote books we think are bad. If we have an affiliate relationship with a book we'd warn against, we drop the affiliate link before we publish the warning.
  • We don't change our reviews based on payout. Higher commissions don't move a book up our list. Lower commissions don't move a book down.
  • We don't hide critical information. If a partner book has a real problem — slow withdrawals, sketchy promo terms, unreliable customer service — we say so, even if it costs us referrals.
  • We don't recommend books we haven't personally evaluated just because they offer affiliate programs. Many sportsbooks pay generous commissions. We recommend the ones we actually use.
  • Books we warn against (like Bovada and Borgata) are warned about based on real experience, not because of any payout structure. We have no affiliate relationship with them either way.

If we ever cross any of those lines, please call us out. Email dr.rob@profitpathsports.com.
