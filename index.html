Books to avoid
Some books have track records that aren't worth the risk — affiliate dollars or not. We don't link these because we don't recommend them.
⚠ Avoid
Bovada

Offshore book operating out of Curaçao — outside U.S. legal protections. If they refuse to pay, there's no realistic path to recovery.

Personal experience

I deposited, claimed a deposit bonus, and got about halfway through the rollover when Bovada limited my account. Bets started showing "under review" — they'd pend for a minute, then the odds would change. Eventually they disabled the account entirely. After five months of emails and support tickets, they re-opened it — but by then the rollover window had passed, so the funds were effectively locked. Never got that money out.

This isn't a one-off. Search matched-betting forums and you'll see the same pattern repeatedly: deposit bonus claimed → halfway through rollover → account limited → funds inaccessible.

If you use them anyway

Deposit small, withdraw fast, and never claim a rollover-attached bonus. Don't keep meaningful balances on the platform.

Strategy Guides
Topics & deep dives
Standalone lessons on specific concepts — outside the structured lanes. Read in any order, return whenever you want a refresher.
🎯
Cross-Market EV CORE STRATEGY
The headline play of modern sports betting. When an arb exists between a soft book and a sharp book, one side HAS to be +EV. Stop hedging — bet only the soft side and capture the full edge.
Read →
🧩
Parlays & SGPs
Sportsbooks make most of their per-bet margin on parlays. Most bettors don't know why. The reason: vig compounds across legs, and same-game parlays add a hidden markup on top. The math, the markup, and when (rarely) parlays actually make sense.
Read →
🪞
Same Bet, Two Prices
"Over 0.5 hits" and "1+ hits" are the exact same bet — but the same book lists them as separate markets at different prices. Always check both when you can.
Read →
🪜
Shopping the Alt Ladder
Books price the Over 1.5 / 2.5 / 3.5 rungs off a model and forget about them. When a rung disagrees with the main line, that's a bet — especially on BetMGM.
Read →
🧭
Top-Down vs. Bottom-Up
There are two completely different routes to +EV — and most profitable bettors only run one without realizing the other exists. The market-trust play vs. the model-builder play. Both work, for opposite reasons. The lesson is figuring out which one you're actually doing.
Read →
⚖️
Probability Gap vs. EV
A 5% probability gap is not 5% expected value. The same edge on a +400 dog returns 4× the EV of the same edge on a −300 favorite. How the two metrics diverge — and which one to bet on.
Read →
📈
EV Betting vs. Stock Trading
Sharp sports betting and quant stock trading run on identical math. The mechanics aren't the same — and one structural difference (books cap winners; exchanges don't) decides whether your edge can scale. Where the two disciplines overlap, where they diverge, and what each can teach the other.
Read →
🔀
Arbitrage Betting
When two books disagree by enough, you can bet both sides and lock guaranteed profit. The math is simple. What trips up most would-be arbers is execution — specifically, what to do when you place one leg and the other vanishes before you can hedge. The full decision tree, plus the play that wins more.
Read →
📅
Futures Betting
A futures ticket isn't a bet you place and forget — it's a position you manage. Why you always split across books, why one must allow cashouts, and the barbell strategy for monetizing mid-season.
Read →
