Lane 02 · New Bettor
Bet Smart From Day One
Four lessons. The foundation every winning bettor builds before placing serious money. Start here if you're new — or if no one ever taught you the math.
01
Know what you're up against: the vig.
Sportsbooks aren't selling you fair odds — they're selling you fair odds plus a tax. Learn to spot the tax on every line before you ever place a bet.
Lesson 01 →
02
The only bet worth placing: +EV.
Most bets lose long-term — by design. A +EV bet is one where the price you're getting beats the true odds. Anything less and you're paying the house to gamble.
EV Calculator →
03
Size every bet correctly.
Even +EV bets lose more than half the time. Kelly tells you how much to risk so variance doesn't blow up your bankroll — and so you actually grow when you win.
Kelly Calculator →
04
Track from day one.
Don't be the bettor who "thinks" they're break-even. Log every bet from your first one, watch your CLV, and know — not guess — whether you're actually +EV.
Bet Tracker →
