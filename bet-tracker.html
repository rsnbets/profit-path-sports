How to use this

Log every bet, track your real edge

  1. Add a bet via the form — date, market, odds, stake, sportsbook, optional notes.
  2. When the bet settles, mark the result (win / loss / push) and the closing line if you have it.
  3. Stats panel updates automatically: ROI, units, win rate, average CLV across your full history.
  4. Filter by date, book, or sport to drill into specific segments and find leaks.
Why this version helps: No account. No signup. No data leaving your browser. Your bet history is yours alone — export to CSV anytime, import the CSV back later. Most paid trackers (Pikkit, etc.) auto-pull from sportsbooks and are excellent — but if you want a private, free, manual-entry option, this is it.

The metric that matters: average CLV. Profit and win-rate are noise short-term. CLV is the only number that doesn't lie.
Data is saved locally in your browser. Clear your cache and it's gone — no account, no backup.
