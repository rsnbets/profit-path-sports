Bankroll & Kelly Sizing
EV Formula: EV = p × (decimal odds − 1) − (1 − p), where p is your true win probability (1 ÷ fair decimal odds). Positive EV means you'll profit on average over many bets — even +1% EV is meaningful when stacked across hundreds of plays. Need fair odds? Strip the vig with the Devig Calculator first.
How to use this

Calculate true EV in 30 seconds

  1. Enter the odds you're being offered at any sportsbook (American or decimal).
  2. Enter the no-vig fair odds — the sharper market's price after stripping vig. Use Devig if you don't have it.
  3. Enter your stake to see EV in dollars (or leave blank for just the percentage).
  4. Read EV % and EV $: positive = +EV (worth placing); negative = skip.
Why this version helps: Most EV calculators give you just the EV percentage. We show EV %, EV $, true win probability, and the Kelly stake all on one screen — so once you confirm a bet is +EV, you immediately know how big to bet without opening a separate tool.

Garbage in, garbage out: EV math is only as good as the no-vig fair odds you enter. Devig the sharp market first.
EV math assumes the fair odds you enter are accurate — garbage in, garbage out. Use the Devig Calculator to compute fair odds from a market.
