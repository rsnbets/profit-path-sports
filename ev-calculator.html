⚡ What This Tool Does
Calculate the true expected value of any bet.
Plug in the odds you're getting, the fair (no-vig) odds, and your stake. The calculator returns true EV % and EV $ — the average amount you'd profit per bet if you placed this same wager thousands of times.
Need fair odds? Use the Devig Calculator to strip the vig out of a 2-way market first. Once you have a no-vig price, drop it into the form above and the EV math is exact.
⚡ EV Formula
EV = p × (decimal odds − 1) − (1 − p), where p is your true win probability (1 ÷ fair decimal odds).
⚡ Positive EV
Means you'll profit on average over many bets. Even +1% EV is meaningful when stacked across hundreds of plays.
⚡ Kelly Sizing
The Kelly bet shows how much of your bankroll to wager based on your edge. Most bettors use ½ or ¼ Kelly to soften variance.
EV math assumes the fair odds you enter are accurate — garbage in, garbage out. Use the Devig Calculator to compute fair odds from a market.
