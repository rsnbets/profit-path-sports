About this book

DraftKings leads the Path 02 starter ladder for one reason: the $5 qualifier is the lowest barrier of any major welcome offer on the market. No deposit gymnastics, no qualifying-bet odds restrictions, no $1,500-or-bust math — just $5 in, four $25 bonus bets out. That's why we put it first. Beyond the offer, DK is one of the two giants in legal US sports betting alongside FanDuel: daily fantasy operator since 2012, full-stack sportsbook in roughly two dozen states. Their core audience is recreational, which means their lines are usually a touch softer than sharp markets like Pinnacle or Bookmaker, and they're more welcoming to casual action.

Where DraftKings shines:

  • Mobile-first app that's genuinely well-designed
  • Wide variety of bet types — props, same-game parlays, futures, every gimmick imaginable
  • Heavy promotional calendar (boosts, parlay insurance, no-sweat bets)
  • Generous and frequent welcome offers for new users

Where it doesn't:

  • Lines can drift from sharp prices — sometimes in your favor, sometimes not
  • They will limit sharp bettors who consistently beat them

If you're new to legal sports betting and want a polished experience to start, DraftKings is a fine first book. Just don't expect them to stay your only book once you're tracking edges across multiple sportsbooks.

What you'll likely get

DraftKings' new-user welcome offer pays out in bonus bets — non-withdrawable credits you have to wager before any profit becomes real cash. The current structure:

Bet $5 → Get 4 × $25 bonus bets ($100 total, regardless of whether your $5 wins).

Exact promo terms can vary by state and rotate occasionally — always read the fine print on the actual signup page before depositing.

What to know about bonus bets:

  • They're non-withdrawable — you have to bet them
  • If a bonus bet wins, only the profit is withdrawable cash (the bonus stake is consumed)
  • Most have 1× rollover (just bet them once) — but read the terms
  • They expire (typically 7-14 days)

That second point is what most people miss. A $25 bonus bet at −110 wins you $23 in cash, not $48. So the trick isn't placing the bonus bet — it's converting its face value into withdrawable money efficiently. The 4 × $25 split is actually an advantage — you get four separate shots at finding good long-odds conversion candidates instead of being locked into one big bet.

How to convert it

Bonus bets are most efficiently converted on long-odds bets where you also hedge the other side at a second sportsbook. The math works out to roughly 70-75% of the bonus bet's face value as real, withdrawable cash. Quick walkthrough:

  1. Place your $5 qualifying bet — anything; it doesn't matter much
  2. Wait for the four $25 bonus bets to land in your account
  3. For each one, find a long-odds bet on DraftKings (e.g. +400 or higher) — see How to find bonus conversion plays for the three ways to surface candidates fast (OddsJam Promo Converter, PTO Premium arb finder, or manual scan)
  4. Use the Bonus Bet Converter to calculate the exact hedge amount
  5. Hedge the opposite side on a second sportsbook at the calculated stake
  6. Repeat for each $25 bonus bet — at ~70% conversion, you walk away with ~$70 in real cash from $5 in
