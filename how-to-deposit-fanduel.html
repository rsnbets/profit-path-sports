How to Deposit Money on FanDuel

Never funded a sportsbook account before? Here's exactly what to tap, what to pick, and what to expect — for total beginners. Takes about 2 minutes.

What you're about to do (60 seconds)

To do anything on FanDuel — place a $5 qualifying bet, claim your free credits, withdraw your winnings — you need money in the account first. The deposit process is similar to topping up any other app on your phone: pick a payment method, type in an amount, confirm. Most people use a debit card or bank account. Money usually shows up in your FanDuel balance within seconds (debit card) or a few hours (bank transfer).

For the Welcome Offer Guide flow, you only need $5 in your FanDuel account. That's all that's needed to place the qualifying bet that unlocks your $200 in free credits. (You'll separately need ~$95 in your ProphetX account for the matching bets, but that's a different deposit on a different site.)

Step-by-step

  1. Open the FanDuel Sportsbook app or website and log in (you should already have an account from Step 1 of the Welcome Offer Guide).
  2. Tap your profile / account icon, usually in the top-right or bottom-right of the screen. Look for a menu item labeled Deposit, Add Funds, or Wallet. On the FanDuel app it's often visible right on the home screen as a "Deposit" button at the top.
  3. Choose a payment method. Your options usually include: debit card (fastest, instant), bank account / ACH (slower, sometimes 1-3 business days, no fees), PayPal (instant if linked), Venmo, online banking like Plaid, or pre-paid options like PayNearMe at retail stores. For your first deposit, debit card is the simplest path.
  4. Enter the amount. Type $10 to $20 — that covers your $5 qualifying bet with some leftover wiggle room (FanDuel sometimes has a $10 minimum deposit, and having a small buffer means you don't get stuck if you want to place a slightly different qualifier).
  5. Confirm the deposit. Tap "Deposit" or "Submit." FanDuel may ask you to verify the payment method the first time (especially with a debit card — they might run a small test charge). Your balance should update within a few seconds.
  6. Confirm your balance. Check the top of the app — you should see your deposited amount as your "Withdrawable" or "Total" balance. If it shows up, you're good to place your $5 qualifying bet.

Common gotchas

  • Your bank might decline the first deposit. Some banks (especially smaller credit unions) treat sportsbook deposits as high-risk by default. If your debit card fails, try a different card, switch to PayPal or online banking (Plaid), or call your bank and ask them to unblock the transaction.
  • Minimum deposit is usually $10. Even though you only need $5 for the qualifier, FanDuel typically requires a $10 minimum to deposit. The extra $5 just sits as withdrawable balance — no harm done.
  • VPNs and out-of-state issues. FanDuel only allows deposits / bets when you're physically located in a legal sports betting state. If you try to deposit while connected to a VPN or while traveling outside a legal state, the app will block it. Turn off the VPN and try again.
  • First deposit might trigger identity verification. If FanDuel asks for a photo of your driver's license or your Social Security number, that's normal — see How to Verify Your Sportsbook Account for the walkthrough.
