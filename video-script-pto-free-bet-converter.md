# PTO Free Bet Converter · Video Script

**Purpose:** Hand-hold a total newbie through using Pick the Odds' free Free Bet Converter to find their first bonus conversion candidate. Embed in `learn-finding-bonus-bets.html` Tier 1 section and `free-money-guide.html` Step 2 once recorded.

**Target runtime:** ~3:25
**Tone:** Confident, friendly, like a friend walking you through it on their laptop
**Audience:** Total newbie · never used PTO before · just signed up at FanDuel + ProphetX through the Free Money Guide

---

## SHOT 1 — OPENING HOOK · ~15 sec

**Screen action:** Brief shot of the PPS Free Money Guide hero on a laptop, then cut to your browser opening to `picktheodds.app`.

**Voiceover:**
> "You signed up for FanDuel, got your free bet credits, and now you need to find a good bet to convert them into real cash. The easiest way to do that is a free tool called Pick the Odds — specifically their Free Bet Converter. Three minutes, I'll show you exactly how to use it."

**On-screen text overlay:** *PTO Free Bet Converter · 3-min walkthrough*

---

## SHOT 2 — SIGN UP AND NAVIGATE · ~25 sec

**Screen action:** Already-logged-in PTO dashboard. Hover over the left sidebar. Click the calculator-style icon (the one for Free Bet Converter).

**Voiceover:**
> "First thing — sign up free at picktheodds.app. No subscription, no credit card. Once you're in, look at the sidebar on the left. The calculator icon is what you want — that's the Free Bet Converter. Click it. Now you're looking at the main screen."

---

## SHOT 2.5 — SET BOOK FILTERS (FanDuel + ProphetX only) · ~30 sec ★ CRITICAL

**Screen action:** From the Free Bet Converter view, locate the book/sportsbook filter (likely a settings gear, a "Sportsbooks" panel, or a filter bar at the top). Open it. Show the full list of books, then deselect everything except **FanDuel** and **ProphetX**. Apply the filter and watch the list shrink dramatically.

**Voiceover:**
> "Before you do anything else, you need to filter the list. Right now PTO is showing you bets across every sportsbook — DraftKings, BetMGM, Caesars, all of them — but for this conversion, we only care about FanDuel and ProphetX. Find the sportsbook filter — it's usually a settings panel or a filter bar at the top. Open it, then deselect every book except **FanDuel** and **ProphetX**. Apply the filter. Watch the list shrink down to just the candidates you can actually use. From here on, every row you see is a real conversion pair you can place."

**On-screen text overlay:** *Filter books: ✓ FanDuel · ✓ ProphetX · uncheck the rest*

---

## SHOT 3 — ORIENTATION · ~20 sec

**Screen action:** Pan across the now-filtered table. Move the cursor over different columns as you mention them. Highlight the candidate count in the corner.

**Voiceover:**
> "Now you're looking at just the conversion pairs that involve FanDuel and ProphetX. Each row is one bet pair: a long-odds bet on FanDuel that turns your free credit into cash, with a matching bet on ProphetX that protects you. Let me show you how to read a single row."

---

## SHOT 4 — READ A ROW · ~45 sec

**Screen action:** Hover on one specific row with a clean conversion % (around 80%, ideally an MLB home run prop or NHL anytime goal scorer to match the lesson examples). Move the cursor through each part as you narrate it.

**Voiceover:**
> "Reading left to right — this row's an MLB game, Detroit Tigers versus the Mets. The bet is a home run prop on a specific player. This icon here — that's the FanDuel side. That's where you'd place your free credit, at these odds. Right under it — the ProphetX side. That's your matching bet, at these opposite odds. PTO already calculated the exact matching bet amount for you, right here. And on the far right — the conversion percentage. That number tells you how much of your free credit you'll keep as real cash. Anything over 75% is solid."

---

## SHOT 5 — SET WAGER + SORT · ~25 sec

**Screen action:** Click into the FanDuel wager field on one row. Type `10` to replace the default. Show the matching bet field automatically update. Then click the conversion % column header to sort high-to-low.

**Voiceover:**
> "For your first conversion, set the wager amount to ten dollars. Just click into the field on the FanDuel side and type ten. Watch the matching bet update automatically — see how it dropped? That keeps your matching bet small, usually under fifty bucks. Then sort by conversion percentage — click the column header. The best candidates float to the top."

**On-screen text overlay:** *Set wager: $10 · Sort by conversion %*

---

## SHOT 6 — PICK A CANDIDATE + VERIFY · ~30 sec

**Screen action:** Click into a top-row candidate at 75%+. Then quick-cut: open FanDuel app/site in a new tab, navigate to the same game, confirm the price. Quick-cut: open ProphetX, find the matching side, confirm.

**Voiceover:**
> "Pick a row above 75 percent. Now here's the important part — switch over to FanDuel and ProphetX directly and check that both prices are still where PTO said they were. Long-odds bets move fast, so always confirm before placing anything. If the prices look the same, you're good. If they've moved a lot, scroll back to PTO and try the next candidate."

---

## SHOT 7 — CLOSING · ~15 sec

**Screen action:** Back on PTO with the chosen row highlighted. End card with the PPS logo and a URL.

**Voiceover:**
> "That's it. Once both prices check out, place your FanDuel bet using free credits, then place the matching bet on ProphetX. Your conversion is locked in. For the exact button-by-button on placing the bets, head back to the Free Money Guide on profitpathsports.com."

**On-screen text overlay:** *Next step: place both bets → profitpathsports.com/free-money-guide.html*

## Recording tips

- **Record one shot at a time** — easier to redo a single bad take than the whole video
- **Slow down on Shot 4** (reading a row) — that's where most newbies pause to absorb. Pace it like you're showing your friend, not racing a clock
- **Capture B-roll on PTO** of just the cursor moving across rows — you can layer this over voiceover gaps in editing
- **Audio matters more than video quality.** Clean voiceover with stock screen capture beats a high-res video with muddy audio every time
- **OBS or QuickTime** for screen capture · iPhone Voice Memos in front of you for clean voiceover (sync in editing) — works fine if you don't have a dedicated mic
- **Pre-stage the screen** — have PTO already logged in with at least 1 good 80%+ candidate visible before you hit record on Shot 4
