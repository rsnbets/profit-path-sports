How the shop works
  1. History finds the spot. A hit rate against the exact line the books hang, not a round number, over the windows that matter.
  2. The price makes the bet. Best book for your side, and the fair price from the two-sided books so you can see what the vig costs.
  3. The matchup and the report sanity-check it. What the opponent allows to the position, ranked 1–32, and the official injury designation.
  4. Then we grade everything. Every prop the feed saw before kickoff is scored against its close. If hot reads don't beat cold ones, we say so.
