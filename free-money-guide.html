4 Steps to Free Money from Sports Books

Every major US sportsbook hands new users $100 to $1,500 in "bonus bets" just for signing up. Most people gamble them away. A small group converts them into guaranteed cash using simple math. Four steps. Roughly $19 in real, withdrawable money on your first conversion — and you've never bet a dollar of real risk to get it.

What this actually is (60 seconds)

Sportsbooks want new customers. To get you in the door, they offer welcome bonuses — usually in the form of bonus bet credits: $25, $50, $150, sometimes up to $1,500 in non-cash credits you can wager.

Here's the trick most people miss: bonus bets are not cash. If you win a $25 bonus bet at +200 odds, you don't get $75 — you get $50 (just the profit, not the stake). And if it loses, the $25 is gone. So the question becomes: how do you turn this non-cash credit into real, withdrawable money?

The answer: place the bonus on a long-odds bet at one book, then place a normal-cash bet on the OPPOSITE outcome at a second book. Whichever side wins, the math works out so you end up with roughly ~70-77% of the bonus value as withdrawable cash. A $25 bonus becomes ~$19 cash. A $150 bonus becomes ~$105 cash. Done across a typical welcome-offer round (4 books, $100-$300 in bonuses each), this clears $300-$500 in real money.

No predictions. No skill. No edge required. The sportsbook is paying you to sign up — you just have to extract the cash mechanically. This is the easiest profit in sports betting, and most people leave it on the table because they don't know it exists.

Before you start — what you need

To do your first conversion you need:

  • ~30 minutes — start to finish, once you've got the accounts set up.
  • Two accounts: FanDuel (where your bonus pool will be) and ProphetX (where you'll place the hedge). You don't have either yet — we'll create both in Step 1.
  • $100 of starter capital — that's the entire budget. $5 goes to your FanDuel qualifying bet (which unlocks your $200 bonus pool), $95 sits on ProphetX as your hedge buffer. That $95 stays parked, redeployed after every conversion as the hedges settle. You're not "spending" it — you're parking it as working capital.
  • A few minutes on a calculator (we provide it free — no math required from you).

That's it. No subscriptions, no scanners, no Excel sheets, no model-building. $100 total in. $150-$200 in withdrawable cash out, after clearing FanDuel's full bonus pool.

Step 2 — Find a long-odds bet on FanDuel with a clean hedge on ProphetX

This is the only part that takes any active scanning. You're looking for a single bet pattern that works every day, in every season, on multiple games. Here's the easiest version:

★ THE TEMPLATE

Pick a sport that's active tonight (NHL, NBA, MLB, NFL, soccer — whichever's in season). You're hunting for one specific shape:

  • On FanDuel (your bonus book): a main-line bet priced between +400 and +700 — typically a heavy moneyline underdog OR a player "anytime scorer / 1+ stat" prop on a low-volume player.
  • On ProphetX (your hedge exchange, same game, same player): the opposite side — the favorite ML, or the player's Under 0.5 stat line — priced around -400 to -600 with real, fillable liquidity (you'll see a stack of orders, not just a quoted spread).

Stick to main lines. Alt versions (alt spreads, alt run lines, "Over 1.5 HRs") often have thin PX liquidity. Main lines always work.

How to actually find one: Open FanDuel and ProphetX side-by-side (browser tabs work well — PX is fully web-based). On FanDuel, scan tonight's games and the Anytime Goal Scorer / Anytime TD Scorer / Player to Hit a HR markets for a low-volume player. Or scan moneylines for a heavy underdog. If FanDuel shows your candidate at +500-ish, switch to PX and check the opposite side around -500 with real liquidity. If the price holds, you have a candidate. If not, try the next one.

★ THE PX LIQUIDITY RULE (TWO RULES, ACTUALLY)

Rule 1 — 2-way markets only. A hedge needs ONE bet that covers the opposite of your bonus leg. That means you need a market with two possible outcomes: Home / Away, Over / Under, Yes / No. Skip 3-way markets like soccer's 3-way moneyline (Home / Draw / Away) or NHL regulation-time 3-way — you'd need to bet two separate sides on PX to fully hedge, which doubles the complexity and rarely sizes cleanly.

Rule 2 — Main lines only. ProphetX is a peer-to-peer exchange — every bet needs real buyers and sellers on both sides. Main game lines (moneyline, standard spread, standard total) and main player-prop lines (the "0.5" over/under for anytime-scorer / 1+ HR / 1+ goal / etc.) have deep PX liquidity because they're where the volume is. Alt lines (alt spreads, alt totals, alt run lines, alt prop lines like "O 1.5 HRs") often have thin or zero PX liquidity — you'll see a price, but it might not actually be available to hit. Always confirm PX shows a real, fillable opposite-side price near fair BEFORE you place the FanDuel bonus leg. If PX doesn't have it cleanly, skip to a different candidate.

Don't overthink which one to pick. The first valid candidate you find is fine. You're not trying to optimize — you're trying to execute. Want the deeper version of this lesson? Read Finding Bonus Conversion Plays. For your first conversion, the patterns above are enough.

Step 4 — Place both bets, then collect

This part is mechanical — but there's one critical thing you must not skip: you have to tell FanDuel to apply the BONUS, not real cash.

  1. On FanDuel: tap the player's Anytime Goal Scorer at +500 to add it to your bet slip. Open the slip. Find the "Bonus Bets" toggle / Apply Bonus button on the slip and select $10 from your bonus pool. FanDuel lets you type any stake amount up to your remaining bonus pool — for this conversion, enter $10. Confirm the slip shows $10 stake (from bonus), +500 odds, $50 to win. Place the bet.
  2. Immediately switch to ProphetX. Find the same player's Under 0.5 Goals in the same game. PX shows an order book with available prices — pick the best available "Buy / Back" price near -500 (one with a real stack behind it, not just a quoted spread) and enter the stake: $41.67. Confirm $41.67 stake (real cash), ~-500 odds, ~$8.33 to win. Confirm the bet matches against existing liquidity (PX shows match status). For first-timers, the PX interface looks different from a regular sportsbook — see the ProphetX walkthrough if you need a visual.
  3. You're done. Both bets are in. The conversion is locked. The game plays out → one of the two bets wins → ~$8.33 in withdrawable cash hits one of your accounts.
