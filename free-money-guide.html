4 Steps to Free Money from Sports Books

Every major US sportsbook hands new users $100 to $1,500 in "bonus bets" just for signing up. Most people gamble them away. A small group converts them into guaranteed cash using simple math. Four steps. Roughly $19 in real, withdrawable money on your first conversion — and you've never bet a dollar of real risk to get it.

What this actually is (60 seconds)

Sportsbooks hand new users bonus bet credits just for signing up — $25 to $1,500, depending on the book. The catch: it's not cash. You can't withdraw it. If the bet loses, the credit's gone; if it wins, you keep only the winnings, not the credit itself.

The play: burn the free credit on a long-odds bet at one site, and at the same time place a cash bet on the OPPOSITE outcome at a second site. The two cancel out — whichever side hits, roughly 70-83% of the credit ends up as real, withdrawable cash. A $10 credit converts to about $8.33. Across a full $200 welcome bonus, that's $150-$200 in real money, mathematically guaranteed.

Before you start — what you need

To do your first conversion you'll need:

  • About 30 minutes. Once your accounts are set up, the actual conversion only takes 5-10 minutes.
  • Two accounts you don't have yet. One at FanDuel (a regular sportsbook — they're going to give you free bet credits to use). One at ProphetX (a different kind of betting site where you'll place a second, "matching" bet that protects your money). Step 1 walks you through creating both.
  • $100 total to start. That's the whole budget. Here's how it breaks down:
    • $5 goes to a tiny first bet at FanDuel. This bet "unlocks" $200 worth of free bet credits — FanDuel's way of saying thanks for signing up.
    • $95 sits in your ProphetX account, ready to be used for the matching bets. You don't lose this money. When each matching bet finishes, the money goes right back to your ProphetX balance, ready to be used again. Think of it as a holding tank — money parked in one place, used over and over.
  • A few minutes on a calculator. We give you a free one — no math from you required.

That's it. No paid subscriptions, no expensive software, no spreadsheets, no math degree. $100 in. By the time you've used up all of FanDuel's $200 in free credits, you'll have $150-$200 in real money you can withdraw to your bank.

Step 1 — Open your two accounts

You're going to create two free accounts. Take 10-15 minutes to set both up before you place any bets. Doing both up front saves you scrambling later.

Account 1 — FanDuel (the sportsbook with the free credits).

Never funded a sportsbook before? See How to Deposit Money on FanDuel for the step-by-step. FanDuel may also ask you to verify your ID — see How to Verify Your Sportsbook Account.
  3. Place a $5 qualifying bet. Pick any game playing today and bet $5 on something simple — like the favorite team's moneyline (the most basic bet: who wins?). It honestly does not matter what you pick or whether the bet wins. The only thing FanDuel cares about is that you placed the $5 bet. That's what "unlocks" your free credits.
    Tip: pick a bet with odds close to even (between -110 and +110, you'll see those numbers next to the bet) so your $5 has roughly a 50/50 chance of winning back. If it wins, great — you just got your $5 back too. If it loses, you're still getting $200 in free credits.

Account 2 — ProphetX (the exchange where you'll place your matching bet).

Step 2 — Find a "long-shot" bet on FanDuel with a matching bet on ProphetX

You're looking for one bet at long odds on FanDuel (between +400 and +700), with the OPPOSITE outcome available on ProphetX at roughly -400 to -600. That's it — same shape every time. The fastest way to find one is the free tool below.

★ THE TEMPLATE (memorize this shape)

Pick any sport that's playing today — basketball (NBA), baseball (MLB), hockey (NHL), football (NFL), whichever's in season. Then find ONE of these two bet shapes:

  • Pattern A — Big underdog moneyline. A bad team playing a good team. The bad team's "moneyline" (the bet that they just win the game outright) will be at long odds: +400, +500, +600, or so. You bet that on FanDuel with free credits.
  • Pattern B — A specific player to "do a thing at least once." Like "Player X to score a touchdown" or "Player Y to hit a home run" or "Player Z to score a goal." Pick a player who doesn't usually do that thing (a backup, a defenseman, a bottom-of-the-order hitter). Their odds will sit around +400 to +700 because it's unlikely. You bet that on FanDuel with free credits.

Then check ProphetX for the opposite bet — the favorite team's moneyline, or "the player does NOT score / hit / etc." Make sure ProphetX actually has it available with real money sitting there to bet against. That's your matching bet.

Step 3 — Decide how much to bet, then use the free calculator

Remember: FanDuel gave you $200 in free credits, and you can use those credits in any amount you want — $10, $25, $50, whatever. For your first conversion, start tiny: just $10 of the credits. Let's say you found a hockey defenseman who's getting +500 odds to score a goal tonight on FanDuel, and ProphetX has the same player at -500 to NOT score with plenty of money waiting on that side. Doesn't matter which side wins — you walk away with about $8.33 in real cash either way. Step 4 — Place both bets, then collect

This part is mechanical — but there's one critical thing you must not skip: when you place the bet on FanDuel, you have to tell it to use your free credits, not your real cash. (FanDuel always defaults to real cash, so this is a step you have to actively take.)

  1. On FanDuel: open the game, find your player, tap the "Anytime Goal Scorer" bet at +500. That adds it to your "bet slip" (the FanDuel term for your bet-in-progress, usually shown as a tab or icon at the bottom of the screen). Open the bet slip. Look near the box where you enter your stake — you'll see something labeled "Bonus Bets" or "Use Bonus Bet" or a small toggle. Tap it and pick "$10" from your available credit pool. Once you've done that, the slip should show: "$10 (Bonus Bet) · +500 odds · $50 to win". Tap "Place Bet."
    This is the highest-stakes step for newbies — placing without activating the bonus credit means betting your own real cash on a long shot. See How to Use a Bonus Bet on FanDuel for the exact button locations and a full screen-by-screen walkthrough.
  2. Immediately switch to ProphetX (don't leave the FanDuel side alone for long — odds can move). Find the same exact player and the same exact bet (the "under 0.5 goals" side this time). ProphetX shows you a list of bets other people have placed at various prices — pick the best available price near -500 that has real money sitting behind it (you'll see dollar amounts next to each price). Enter your stake: $41.67. The screen should confirm: "$41.67 cash · -500 odds · $8.33 to win." Tap "Place Bet" and wait for it to confirm that the bet "matched" (which just means another user's bet on the other side accepted yours).
    Quick note: ProphetX's screen looks different from a regular sportsbook because it shows multiple prices side-by-side instead of just one. If it feels confusing the first time, our ProphetX walkthrough page has a visual breakdown.
  3. Both bets are placed. You're done. Now you just wait for the game to play out. One of your two bets will win, the other will lose, and about $8.33 in real cash will hit one of your accounts. The math is locked in — there's nothing more for you to do.
⚠ THE ONE MISTAKE TO AVOID

Before you tap "Place Bet" on FanDuel, double-check the bet slip says "Bonus Bet" right next to the dollar amount. If it doesn't, you forgot to activate the free credit — which means you're about to bet $10 of YOUR real money on a long-shot bet that almost certainly loses. The entire conversion only works when FanDuel pays for the bet with their credits, not your wallet. Look at the slip. If you don't see the word "Bonus" somewhere, fix it before placing.
