4 Steps to Free Money from Sports Books

Every major US sportsbook hands new users $100 to $1,500 in "bonus bets" just for signing up. Most people gamble them away. A small group converts them into guaranteed cash using simple math. Four steps. Roughly $19 in real, withdrawable money on your first conversion — and you've never bet a dollar of real risk to get it.

What this actually is (60 seconds)

Sportsbooks want new customers. To get you in the door, they offer welcome bonuses — usually in the form of bonus bet credits: $25, $50, $150, sometimes up to $1,500 in non-cash credits you can wager.

Here's the trick most people miss: bonus bets are not cash. If you win a $25 bonus bet at +200 odds, you don't get $75 — you get $50 (just the profit, not the stake). And if it loses, the $25 is gone. So the question becomes: how do you turn this non-cash credit into real, withdrawable money?

The answer: place the bonus on a long-odds bet at one book, then place a normal-cash bet on the OPPOSITE outcome at a second book. Whichever side wins, the math works out so you end up with roughly ~70-77% of the bonus value as withdrawable cash. A $25 bonus becomes ~$19 cash. A $150 bonus becomes ~$105 cash. Done across a typical welcome-offer round (4 books, $100-$300 in bonuses each), this clears $300-$500 in real money.

No predictions. No skill. No edge required. The sportsbook is paying you to sign up — you just have to extract the cash mechanically. This is the easiest profit in sports betting, and most people leave it on the table because they don't know it exists.

Before you start — what you need

To do your first conversion you need:

  • ~30 minutes — start to finish, once you've got the accounts set up.
  • A DraftKings account (you don't have one yet — we'll create it in Step 1).
  • A second sportsbook account for the hedge — FanDuel is the easiest pair. You'll also pick up its welcome bonus while you're at it. Free money on top of free money.
  • About $110-$150 in your hedge book account, in real cash. This funds the hedge bet. You'll get it back (plus profit, or replaced by bonus winnings) — it's not at risk; it's just temporarily parked.
  • A few minutes on a calculator (we provide it free — no math required from you).

That's it. No subscriptions, no scanners, no Excel sheets, no model-building. The infrastructure cost is one ~$5 qualifying bet at DraftKings and ~$110 of hedge stake that you'll get back.

Step 2 — Find a long-odds bet on DK with a clean hedge on FanDuel

This is the only part that takes any active scanning. You're looking for a single bet pattern that works every day, in every season, on multiple games. Here's the easiest version:

★ THE TEMPLATE

Pick a single MLB game tonight (or NHL, NBA, NFL — whatever's in season). Look at the alt run line / alt spread / alt puck line markets. You're hunting for one specific shape:

  • On DraftKings: the underdog at +2.5 runs (MLB), +12.5 or higher (NBA/NFL), or +1.5 puck line (NHL) — priced anywhere between +400 and +700.
  • On FanDuel (same game, same market): the favorite at -2.5 runs / etc. — priced between -500 and -700.

Both books must have the same game and the same number. If DK has it at +2.5 runs but FanDuel only shows +1.5 or +3.5, that's a different bet — skip it and find another game.

How to actually find one: Open the DraftKings app and FanDuel app side-by-side (or in two browser tabs). Scan the MLB game list. Tap into the Alt Run Lines for any game with a heavy favorite. If DK shows the dog at +500-ish and FanDuel shows the favorite at -550-ish, you have a candidate. If not, check the next game. There's usually one every night during MLB season.

Other patterns that work:

  • NBA: alt total way off the standard line (e.g. if the standard total is 224, look at the over at 233.5 priced +500 or so)
  • NFL: alt spread on the dog by 13+ points
  • NHL: alt puck line on the dog by +2.5 goals (less common — most games are -1.5/+1.5)

Don't overthink which one to pick. The first valid candidate you find is fine. You're not trying to optimize — you're trying to execute. Want the deeper version of this lesson? Read Finding Bonus Conversion Plays. For your first conversion, the template above is enough.

Step 4 — Place both bets, then collect

This part is mechanical — but there's one critical thing you must not skip: you have to tell DraftKings to apply the BONUS, not real cash.

  1. On DraftKings: tap Tigers +2.5 at +500 to add it to your bet slip. Open the slip. Find the "Use Bonus Bet" toggle near the stake amount and turn it on. Select the $25 bonus you want to apply. Confirm the slip shows $25 stake (from bonus), +500 odds, $125 to win. Place the bet.
  2. Immediately switch to FanDuel. Tap Yankees -2.5 at -550 to add it to your bet slip. Open the slip. Enter the exact hedge stake from the Bonus Converter: $105.74. Confirm $105.74 stake (real cash), -550 odds, $19.22 to win. Place the bet.
  3. You're done. Both bets are in. The conversion is locked. The game plays out → one of the two bets wins → ~$19 in withdrawable cash hits one of your accounts.
⚠ DO NOT FORGET

Toggle "Use Bonus Bet" on DraftKings BEFORE you place the long-odds bet. If you don't, you'll bet $25 of your own cash on a +500 long shot — that's a bad bet on its own. The whole conversion only works when DK is paying with bonus credit, not your wallet. Check the slip says "Bonus Bet" before tapping Place Bet.
