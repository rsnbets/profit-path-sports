Step-by-Step: Convert Your First Bonus Bet

The click-by-click walkthrough of executing one bonus conversion end-to-end. We'll work through a realistic example: $25 DraftKings bonus bet → long-odds alt run line on DK → hedge on FanDuel → ~$19 in withdrawable cash. Every step, every screen, every gotcha.

Before you start — quick checklist

Before we walk through a real conversion, make sure you have all of this in place. Missing any one of these and you'll get stuck mid-process — much less stressful to confirm up front than to discover at step 4.

  • Bonus bet visible in your bonus book account. Open the app/site, navigate to your account, and confirm the bonus bets are sitting there. The actual button to apply them looks different on every book — find it now so you're not hunting later.
  • Second account funded with real cash. The hedge leg is paid with real money, not bonus credit. You need enough cash on the hedge book to cover the calculated hedge stake (we'll see — for a $25 bonus at +500 odds, the hedge stake is around $105).
  • Both books open in separate tabs. Speed matters — lines can move between tabs. If you're on mobile, have both apps open and ready to switch fast.
  • The Bonus Converter open in a third tab. You'll plug numbers into it before placing the second leg.
  • A bet tracker ready — our free tracker, Pikkit, or a spreadsheet. You're going to log this immediately so you don't lose track of what's hedging what.

Don't skip the checklist. Bonus conversion is mechanical, but the failure modes are all on the execution side. Forgetting which button toggles "use bonus bet" mid-flow has cost real bettors real money. Set up cleanly.

Our worked example

For this walkthrough we'll convert one $25 DraftKings bonus bet end-to-end. The example is generic enough that the same pattern applies to any bonus book and any sport:

The play Game: MLB · Tigers @ Yankees (regular season) Bonus book: DraftKings $25 bonus bet credit Bonus side: Tigers +2.5 runs @ +500 (alt run line) Hedge book: FanDuel real cash Hedge side: Yankees -2.5 runs @ -550 Bonus Converter → hedge stake $105.74 Locked-in cash: $19.24 (~77% conversion of $25)

This is exactly the type of candidate the Finding Bonus Conversion Plays lesson teaches you to spot. Heavy underdog +2.5 / +3.5 run lines on MLB, NHL puck lines, NBA / NFL alt spreads at the +400-+700 end are the cleanest patterns. The hedge book gets the favorite side at modest juice; you get the long-odds side on the bonus book.

Now we execute.

Step 1 — Confirm the bonus bet is actually in your account

Open DraftKings. Find your bonus bet credits. On the DK app this lives under Account → Promotions → Available Rewards, or you'll see them stacked at the top of your bet slip whenever you build a bet. You should see a clearly-labeled "$25 Bonus Bet" entry.

If the bonus bet isn't there yet (and you've already met the qualifying-bet conditions), give DK a few hours — credits don't always post instantly after the qualifier settles. Don't proceed without confirming the credit exists; placing the long-odds bet with real cash defeats the entire conversion strategy.

📷 Screenshot placeholder: DK app showing the bonus bet credit visible in the bet slip drawer. (Coming in v2 of this lesson.)

Step 2 — Find your conversion pair

You need a long-odds bet on DraftKings (between +400 and +900) where the opposite side is priced cleanly on FanDuel (or another funded hedge book). The Finding Bonus Conversion Plays lesson covers three methods (OddsJam Promo Converter, PTO Premium arb finder, manual scan).

For our example, the pair is:

  • DK: Tigers +2.5 runs at +500
  • FanDuel: Yankees -2.5 runs at -550

Both pricing on real, liquid MLB run lines. Not props, not parlays, not futures. Game lines only.

Once you've identified the pair, don't place anything yet. Lines move. Step 3 verifies they're still there.

Step 3 — Verify availability on both books, right now

This is the step most bettors skip — and it's where conversions go wrong. Markets move. A scanner found your pair 10 minutes ago, but if DK's +500 has shifted to +475 or FanDuel's -550 has crept to -625, your conversion math is now different (and probably worse).

Quick verification flow:

  1. Open the DK game page. Navigate: MLB → Tigers @ Yankees → Run Line / Alt Run Lines → +2.5 runs. Confirm the price is still +500 (or close — within 25-50 cents is workable; re-run the math at the actual current price).
  2. Switch to the FanDuel tab. Navigate: same game → Run Line / Alt Run Lines → Yankees -2.5. Confirm -550 (or close).
  3. If either has moved meaningfully, either re-run the Bonus Converter with the new prices, or find a different pair. Don't force a stale candidate.

Once both sides are confirmed live, you have maybe 30-90 seconds before the next line movement. Don't dawdle. Place both legs back-to-back.

📷 Screenshot placeholder: Side-by-side view of DK at +500 and FanDuel at -550 for the same alt run line.

Step 4 — Place the bonus bet on DraftKings

On DK with your Tigers +2.5 page open:

  1. Tap +500 next to "Tigers +2.5 Runs." This adds it to your bet slip.
  2. Open the bet slip drawer. You should see Tigers +2.5 at +500 sitting there.
  3. Toggle "Use Bonus Bet" — this is the critical step. On DK there's a checkbox or pill near the stake field labeled Bonus Bet. Tap it and select the $25 bonus you want to apply. If you don't toggle this, you'll bet $25 of your own cash and waste the bonus.
  4. Confirm the slip shows:
    • Stake: $25 (from bonus, not cash)
    • Odds: +500
    • To Win: $125 (bonus bets pay profit only — you don't get the $25 stake back if it wins)
  5. Place the bet. Wait for confirmation that it settled into your open bets.

That leg is now locked. The $25 bonus credit is consumed regardless of outcome. Your obligation now: place the hedge before the FanDuel line moves.

📷 Screenshot placeholder: DK bet slip with "Use Bonus Bet" toggled on, showing $25 stake → $125 to win.

Step 5 — Run the Bonus Converter math

Before you place the hedge, you need the exact hedge stake. Open the Bonus Bet Converter in your third tab and enter:

  • Bonus bet value: $25
  • Bonus bet odds: +500 (American)
  • Hedge book odds: -550 (American)

The tool returns:

Converter output Recommended hedge stake: $105.74 If Tigers cover (+2.5): bonus pays +$125.00 hedge loses -$105.74 net cash: +$19.26 If Yankees cover (-2.5): bonus loses $0 (no stake back) hedge pays +$19.22 net cash: +$19.22 Locked-in conversion: ~$19.24 (77% of $25 bonus)

The two outcome scenarios cash within a few cents of each other — that's what "locked-in conversion" means. You're not predicting whether Tigers or Yankees covers; you're guaranteed the same ~$19 either way, off zero cash exposure on the bonus side and a $105.74 hedge stake that you'll get back (plus profit, or replaced by bonus winnings).

Round the hedge stake to a number FanDuel will accept (most books accept $0.01 increments now, so $105.74 is fine).

Step 6 — Place the hedge on FanDuel

Switch to your FanDuel tab. The line should still be -550 (you verified in Step 3 — but glance again, it could have moved in the last 60 seconds).

  1. Tap -550 next to "Yankees -2.5 Runs." Adds to your bet slip.
  2. Open the bet slip. Confirm Yankees -2.5 at -550.
  3. Enter the exact hedge stake from Step 5: $105.74. Don't round to $100 or $110 — that throws off your locked-in cash.
  4. Confirm the slip shows:
    • Stake: $105.74 (real cash, not bonus)
    • Odds: -550
    • To Win: $19.22 (matches our converter math)
    • Total Payout: $124.96 (stake + winnings, if hedge wins)
  5. Place the bet. Wait for confirmation.

Both legs are now in. The conversion is locked. You're guaranteed $19.24 in withdrawable cash, regardless of whether Tigers or Yankees covers the run line.

📷 Screenshot placeholder: FanDuel bet slip with Yankees -2.5 at -550, stake $105.74, to win $19.22.

Step 7 — Two outcomes, same cash

The game plays out. One of two things happens:

Outcome A · Tigers cover
Final: Yankees win by 1 or 2 (or Tigers win)
Bonus bet wins: +$125 cash from DK
Hedge loses: -$105.74 on FanDuel
Net cash: +$19.26
Outcome B · Yankees cover
Final: Yankees win by 3 or more
Bonus bet loses: $0 (no stake refund on bonus bets)
Hedge wins: +$19.22 profit on FanDuel
Net cash: +$19.22

Either way, ~$19.24 in withdrawable cash hits one of your accounts. The bonus credit converted into real money. You didn't predict an outcome — you arbitraged the gap between DK's long-odds bonus pricing and FanDuel's tight hedge pricing.

That's it. One conversion, ~$19 cash, off about 5 minutes of execution time once you've done it once or twice. Repeat for every bonus bet in your stack. Four $25 bonus bets across a welcome-offer round = ~$77 cash without any predictive skill required.

Common mistakes that ruin a conversion

Six failure modes that have actually cost real bettors real money. Avoid them all:

  1. Forgot to toggle "Use Bonus Bet." Placed the long-odds bet with cash instead of credit. Now you're betting $25 of real money on a +500 underdog, hedged. That's a negative-EV arb after vig — guaranteed loss. Always confirm the bonus-bet pill is active on the slip before placing.
  2. Skipped the live verification (Step 3). Scanner showed +500/-550 ten minutes ago; by the time you placed, DK had moved to +450 and FanDuel to -625. Your conversion is now well below expected — sometimes net negative. Always re-check both prices the second before placing.
  3. Hedge stake rounded badly. The Converter said $105.74; you placed $100 because you wanted round numbers. You're now mis-hedged: cash on one outcome is much better than the other. The locked-in conversion is gone. Use the exact stake.
  4. Forgot to place the hedge entirely. Got distracted between legs, never went back to FanDuel. Now you have a naked +500 bet sitting in DK with no hedge — pure variance. Place both legs in one sitting; never walk away mid-flow.
  5. Used a market without a clean hedge. Tried to convert on a player prop, a parlay, or a future. The other side either isn't available on your hedge book or carries 25%+ juice. Conversion math falls apart. Game lines only.
  6. Didn't track it. Two weeks later, you can't remember which conversion was hedged where. Did the hedge settle? Did the bonus credit clear? Log every conversion immediately in your tracker with both leg odds, stakes, and expected cash. Settles automatically when both legs grade.

Track it before you move on

Right after placing both legs, open your Bet Tracker (or Pikkit if you've linked your books) and log the pair as a single conversion entry. Standard fields:

  • Date / game / market — Tigers @ Yankees, MLB, alt run line
  • Bonus leg: DK · Tigers +2.5 · +500 · $25 bonus
  • Hedge leg: FanDuel · Yankees -2.5 · -550 · $105.74 cash
  • Expected cash: $19.24
  • Tag: "Bonus conversion" (separate from your +EV bet log)

Tag conversions as their own category. They behave differently from +EV bets — the cash is locked in and not subject to variance — so don't let them inflate or pollute your normal +EV ROI math. Many trackers will let you filter by tag; conversion ROI lives in its own bucket.

Once tracked, withdraw or hold the cash according to your bankroll plan. You're done with this conversion. Repeat for the next bonus bet.

Once you've done one, you've done them all

Bonus conversion is the most mechanical, repeatable, predictable play in sports betting. There's no edge to find, no model to build, no game-day decision to make. You're not betting on Tigers or Yankees — you're harvesting a fixed gap between bonus-bet pricing and real-money hedge pricing.

The first one feels slow because you're checking the math three times. The second one feels normal. By the fourth or fifth, you'll execute the whole flow in 3 minutes from "find candidate" through "both legs placed." Across a typical 4-book welcome-offer stack with multiple bonus bets each, you can clear $300-500 in pure cash without taking any meaningful risk.

That cash is the seed capital for everything that comes after — Path 03's +EV bankroll, DFS allocation if you go there, etc. The bonus round is where your real money comes from. The rest is variance.
