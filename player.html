How to use the prop finder

  1. Search a player, pick a prop. The line chips are tonight's actual posted lines — best Over and Under price across every book we track, with the vig-free fair price beside each.
  2. Read the clear rates. Season is the anchor; L15/L10/L5 show form; vs Opp shows the head-to-head — with the sample size printed right on it, because three games is a coin flip, not a pattern.
  3. Use the − / + stepper to test any threshold. His whole history recomputes instantly — useful for alt lines the books haven't posted yet.
  4. Check the chart. Bars are his actual game-by-game numbers; the dotted line is the threshold. Green cleared it, red didn't.
  5. Splits and head-to-head are context, not verdicts. A 6-for-30 vs lefties or a 2-for-9 against tonight's starter is trivia until the sample gets real.
The order matters: the market's fair price is the market's answer; his history is your evidence for disagreeing with it. If they agree, there's no bet — no matter how pretty the clear rate looks.
