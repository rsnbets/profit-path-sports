How to use this

Translate any line into plain language

  1. Enter American odds in the left field (e.g. −175 or +135).
  2. The right field updates with the implied probability — the win-rate the price is asking you to beat.
  3. Conversion runs both ways — type a probability (63.64%) on the right and the American odds update on the left.
Why this version helps: Once you see −110 as "52.38% break-even," every line on a sportsbook reads differently. This is the simplest tool on the site — but it's also the one that flips how you read odds for every other lesson and calc.
