How to use this

What it does. Converts a betting price between the four formats sportsbooks and bettors use — American, decimal, fractional, and implied probability — live, in any direction. Edit any field and the other three recalculate instantly.

The four formats.

  • American — the +/− number US books show. +150 means a $100 bet profits $150. −150 means you risk $150 to profit $100.
  • Decimal — total return per $1 staked, stake included. 2.50 returns $2.50 for every $1. Standard in Europe and on betting exchanges.
  • Fractional — profit-to-stake ratio. 3/2 means win $3 profit for every $2 risked. The traditional UK format.
  • Implied probability — the win rate the price is asking you to beat. The most useful number of the four: it's what devig, EV, and CLV all run on.

How to use it. Type into whichever field you have a number for — the other three follow. Or tap a Quick Reference tile to load a common line and see all four formats at once.

Why implied probability matters. Once you read −110 as "52.38% break-even" instead of just a number, every line on a sportsbook reads differently. Converting price into probability is the single move behind every other tool on this site — it's worth running until it's automatic.

Note. Decimal and fractional formats here describe the raw price only — they don't strip the bookmaker's vig. To find the fair probability behind a two-sided market, run it through the Devig Calculator or Bet X-Ray.
