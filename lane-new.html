The Fundamentals

Six short lessons — odds, implied probability, the vig, +EV, Kelly, and CLV. The math every winning bettor runs daily. Nothing more, nothing less. Most bettors think they already know this. Most bettors are losing — the two facts are related.

01
Read the odds
−115, +105, decimal, fractional — what every number on a sportsbook actually means. Once you can read a moneyline, you can read every bet on the board.
Lesson 01 →
02
Translate odds into probability
Every price is a percentage in disguise. See the probability behind the line and you stop thinking in dollars and start thinking like a sharp.
Lesson 02 →
03
Know what you're up against — the vig
Sportsbooks don't sell fair odds — they sell fair odds plus a tax. Learn to spot that tax on every line before you ever place a bet.
Lesson 03 →
04
The only bet worth placing — +EV
Most bets lose long-term by design. A +EV bet is one where the price you get beats the true odds. Anything less and you're paying the house to gamble.
Lesson 04 →
05
Size every bet correctly
Even +EV bets lose more than half the time. Kelly tells you how much to risk so variance doesn't blow up your bankroll — and so you actually grow when you win.
Lesson 05 →
06
Track from day one
Don't be the bettor who "thinks" they're break-even. Log every bet, watch your CLV, and know — not guess — whether you're actually +EV.
Lesson 06 →
