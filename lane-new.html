The Fundamentals

Six short lessons on odds, vig, EV, Kelly, and CLV — the math every winning bettor runs daily. Most losing bettors think they already know this and they don't. The gap between recognizing the concepts and actually using them is what separates the two groups. Skip what you've got down. Learn the rest cold.

01
Read the odds.
−115, +105, decimal odds, dollar payouts — what every number on a sportsbook actually means. Once you can read a moneyline, you can read every bet on the board.
Lesson 01 →
02
Translate odds into probability.
Every odds price is a percentage in disguise. Once you can see the probability behind the line, you stop thinking in dollars and start thinking like a sharp.
Lesson 02 →
03
Know what you're up against: the vig.
Sportsbooks aren't selling you fair odds — they're selling you fair odds plus a tax. Learn to spot the tax on every line before you ever place a bet.
Lesson 03 →
04
The only bet worth placing: +EV.
Most bets lose long-term — by design. A +EV bet is one where the price you're getting beats the true odds. Anything less and you're paying the house to gamble.
Lesson 04 →
05
Size every bet correctly.
Even +EV bets lose more than half the time. Kelly tells you how much to risk so variance doesn't blow up your bankroll — and so you actually grow when you win.
Lesson 05 →
06
Track from day one.
Don't be the bettor who "thinks" they're break-even. Log every bet from your first one, watch your CLV, and know — not guess — whether you're actually +EV.
Lesson 06 →
