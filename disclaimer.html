Responsible gambling

Gambling can be addictive. If gambling is causing problems in your life, please stop and seek help. It is never worth it.

Signs of a gambling problem may include: betting more than you can afford to lose, chasing losses, lying about gambling, neglecting work or family, borrowing money to gamble, feeling unable to stop. If any of these apply, please reach out to one of the resources below.

Problem gambling resources

  • National Council on Problem Gambling (NCPG): ncpgambling.org · Helpline: 1-800-GAMBLER (1-800-426-2537) · Text: 800GAM
  • Gamblers Anonymous: gamblersanonymous.org · Peer-support recovery groups, free, available in most U.S. states.
  • SAMHSA National Helpline (U.S.): 1-800-662-HELP (4357) · 24/7 free and confidential treatment referral.
  • Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741 (U.S./Canada) for free 24/7 crisis support.
  • GamCare (UK): gamcare.org.uk · Helpline: 0808 8020 133.
  • Many states also offer state-funded problem-gambling helplines. Search "[your state] problem gambling helpline" to find local resources.

If you or someone you know is struggling with gambling, the most important step is reaching out. The resources above are free, confidential, and staffed by people trained to help. You are not alone, and recovery is possible.
