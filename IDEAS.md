## Concept Hooks — "Bird in the hand" pattern

The pattern: a familiar phrase that does 90% of the conceptual work for the reader before any explanation. *"A bird in the hand is worth two in the bush"* for hedging is the gold standard — we want one of these per concept/calc.

### Hedging ✓ (locked in)
- **"Sometimes a bird in the hand is worth two in the bush."**

### EV (Expected Value)
- ★ **"It's not the bet — it's the bets."**
- "Win the math, even when you lose the bet."
- "The house wasn't built on one hand."
- "You can lose the hand and still be right."

### Vig
- ★ **"Death, taxes, and vig."**
- "The house always gets paid first."
- "The cover charge for being at the bar."
- "Nobody plays for free."

### Devig
- ★ **"What the line would say if no one was watching."**
- "Strip the math, see the truth."
- "Find the price before the markup."

### CLV (Closing Line Value)
- ★ **"The score doesn't matter. The closing line does."**
- "The game's over before the game starts."
- "Past the bell. Past the noise."

### Kelly / Bet Sizing
- ★ **"Bet too little, miss the ride. Bet too much, miss the ride."**
- "How much to bet matters more than what to bet."
- "Half the math. Twice the survival." *(specifically for half-Kelly)*

### Arbitrage
- ★ **"Don't pick a side. Pick both."**
- "Two prices, one outcome, zero risk."
- "Free money exists. Briefly."

### Middle
- ★ **"Two roads. One lottery ticket."**
- "Win the bet. Win the hedge. Win the middle."

### Bonus Conversion
- ★ **"Make the freeroll real."**
- "The book gave you a coupon. Cash the coupon."
- "Free money is rarely free, and rarely money."

### Manual Scan / Cross-Market Edge
- ★ **"Three books. Three prices. Pick the soft one."**
- "The market is a chorus. Find the off-key note."

### Maker Tactic (Exchange)
- ★ **"If you don't like the line, write your own."**
- "Stop taking the price. Start setting it."
- "Be the bookmaker."

### Translation Rule (Exchange)
- ★ **"The price on the screen isn't an offer. It's a dare."**
- "Every offer is the other guy's confession."
- "Read the trap. Take the bet."

### Walls (Exchange signal)
- ★ **"Walls go up where the smart money parked."**
- "Read the wall. Bet the other side."

### Bet Tracker
- ★ **"If you didn't track it, it didn't happen."**
- "You can't fix what you don't see."
- "The book of you."

### Long-Run Simulator
- ★ **"Variance is what skill feels like."**
- "Show me ten thousand seasons."
- "The long run isn't a number — it's a graph."

### Sharp Snapshot (calculator)
- ★ **"What the smart money is doing, on one screen."**

### Parlay Calculator

- "The book's favorite bet is your favorite bet."
- "Multiply your odds. Multiply the vig."
