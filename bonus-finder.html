How to use the Conversion Finder

  1. Enter your bonus amount and pick the book where the bonus credit is sitting.
  2. The finder scans the same live MLB odds that power our other tools and lists bets where your book has one side and a different book has the other side.
  3. Each row shows the full recipe: place the bonus bet on the listed side at your book, then place the cash hedge for the exact dollar amount shown at the hedge book. Either way the bet settles, you keep the locked profit.
  4. Rows are ranked by conversion rate — locked profit as a % of the bonus. 65–80% is an excellent conversion; anything above that usually means a line moved, so re-check the odds at both books before betting.
  5. Click any row to see the payout math for both outcomes plus backup hedge books.
Why longshots? A bonus bet doesn't return your stake — only winnings. The plus-money side wastes less of the bonus on stake, so higher bonus-side odds convert more of the credit into cash. That's why the list defaults to +150 and up.
Before you bet: odds move fast — always confirm both prices in the apps. These are player props, so check void rules: if the player is scratched, most books void both sides, but rules differ. Want to run your own numbers? Use the Bonus Bet Converter.
