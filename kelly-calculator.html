Enter your edge and odds to calculate
How Kelly works: f* = (bp − q) / b Where b = decimal odds − 1, p = your true win probability, q = 1 − p. Full Kelly maximizes long-term bankroll growth but with high variance. Most pros use ¼ or ½ Kelly to reduce drawdowns. If your edge is negative, the calculator will tell you not to bet.
How to use this

Find your Kelly stake

  1. Enter your current bankroll.
  2. Enter the odds of the bet (American or decimal).
  3. Enter your estimated true probability of winning — the no-vig fair probability. Use the Devig Calculator if you don't have it.
  4. Read Full / ½ / ¼ Kelly stakes side-by-side.
Why this version helps: Most Kelly calculators give you one number. We show full, half, and quarter Kelly at once so you can pick the variance tolerance you can actually live with. Full Kelly maximizes growth but with brutal swings; quarter Kelly gives ~75% of the long-run growth with about a third of the volatility — and most pros run quarter or half.
