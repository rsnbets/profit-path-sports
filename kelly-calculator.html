Kelly Calculator

The right bet size grows your bankroll fastest — without blowing it up. Edge + odds + bankroll → the Kelly-optimal stake. Full, three-quarter, half, or quarter Kelly — pick the variance you can live with.

In plain English

Bet too little on a good bet and you barely grow. Bet too much and one bad run wipes you out. This tool does the math on how much to put down — big enough to grow your money, small enough to survive the losing streaks.

★ Key Point — Full Kelly Lies to You
The Kelly formula assumes your win probability is exact. It isn't — it's an estimate. Bet full Kelly on an estimate that's even a little too high and you're badly over-betting, which drains a bankroll even when the edge is real. That's why serious bettors use half or quarter Kelly — the haircut buys a margin for the error in your own number. When in doubt, bet less. New here? The lesson Kelly Criterion walks through it.
How to use this

What it does. Takes your edge and bankroll and returns the stake that grows your money the fastest over the long run — the Kelly-optimal bet — then lets you dial it down to a safer fraction.

How to use it.

  • Pick an input mode. Edge (EV %) — paste the EV% straight from the EV Calculator. Win probability — enter your estimated true (no-vig) win rate instead; devig a market if you need it.
  • Enter your bankroll and the odds you can bet (American or decimal).
  • Pick a Kelly fraction. Half is the default and the right starting point for most bettors.
  • Read the Recommended Stake — and never bet more than the Full-Kelly Reference.

The fractions.

  • Quarter — conservative. ~44% of full Kelly's growth, but far smoother — the most survivable through cold streaks.
  • Half — the working-pro default. ~75% of full Kelly's growth with roughly half the swings.
  • Three-Quarter — aggressive. High growth, deep drawdowns.
  • Full — theoretically optimal growth, but only if your win probability is exactly right. It never is.
