Path 02 · Bonus Hunter

Building Your Bankroll

A practical sequence — which two books first, when to add the next, and why every new book multiplies your conversion power.

Why you start with two books, not one

You can't convert bonus bets with a single sportsbook. The whole play depends on placing your bonus bet on one side at Book A, and hedging the other side with cash at Book B. That's the absolute minimum.

The recommended starting pair: one recreational US sportsbook (your "offer book" — where the welcome bonus comes from) and one exchange or sharp book (your "lay book" — where you place the cash hedge with tight pricing).

The starting pair: Caesars + ProphetX

For the very first conversion, this is the friendliest pairing on the page:

Offer book
Caesars
$25 referral bonus is small enough that nothing goes catastrophically wrong on your first try. Easy app, simple workflow, US-licensed.
Lay book
ProphetX
Peer-to-peer exchange — tight pricing, no vig on the hedge side, and you can place big hedges without getting flagged or limited.

Why these two specifically? Caesars' referral bonus is modest ($25), so the dollar stakes are low while you nail down the workflow. ProphetX gives you the cleanest hedge prices you can find — and the bigger you eventually scale your hedges, the more this matters.

The hedge-size edge. Recreational books (DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM) actively flag and limit accounts that place consistent large hedges — it looks like sharp behavior to them, and they'll cut your max stake or close your account entirely. Exchanges work differently: ProphetX makes money on commission, not by beating you on the line. They don't care if you bet $50 or $5,000 — same pricing, no flags, no limits. When you graduate from $25 Caesars conversions to $1,000+ BetMGM first-bet conversions, hedging that size at a recreational book is risky. At ProphetX, it's just another bet.

Your first conversion, step by step

  1. Sign up at Caesars through the referral link. Deposit $25.
  2. Sign up at ProphetX with your referral code. Deposit $50-100 to fund the hedge.
  3. Place your $25 qualifying bet at Caesars (something safe — a heavy moneyline favorite is fine). Wait for it to settle.
  4. Once the bonus bets land in your Caesars account, find a long-odds market — say a +400 underdog moneyline.
  5. Open the Bonus Bet Converter, plug in $25 bonus + the +400 line, and it tells you the exact hedge stake.
  6. Place the hedge on the OPPOSITE side at ProphetX (the favorite, around −400, in this example).
  7. Whichever side wins, the math works out: roughly ~$18 in real, withdrawable cash.

Pro tip on the qualifier. Step 3 above says "bet something safe" — and you can do better. Find an arb or low-hold opportunity across two books and bet both sides for the qualifier. An arb is when the two prices' implied probabilities sum to less than 100% (guaranteed profit regardless of who wins). A low-hold is when they sum to just above 100% — you lose only a fraction of a percent instead of the typical 4-5% vig. Tools like OddsJam scan for these in real time. Your qualifying bet stops being an expected loss and becomes break-even-or-better — pure upside on the bonus bets that follow.

$18 isn't life-changing. But you've now run the playbook end-to-end with low risk. The workflow doesn't change when the numbers get bigger — only the stakes do.

Add the next book — and why it's worth more

Now open Book #3. Doesn't matter much which — a major US book like DraftKings, FanDuel, or BetMGM. Run their welcome offer the same way.

Here's where layering pays off: with three books, you suddenly have three places to shop the hedge price. Instead of always hedging on ProphetX, you check ProphetX, Caesars, AND DraftKings — and place the hedge wherever the line is sharpest.

Hedging a +180 Knicks bonus bet — Lakers ML at three books ProphetX: −160 (tightest)
Caesars: −175 (vig'd up)
DraftKings: −150 (off-market in your favor)

Hedge at DraftKings → conversion ratio jumps

Each additional book is another shot at finding a loose line on the hedge side. Two books gets you ~70% conversion. Five books gets ~74%. Eight books — the full-stack matched bettor setup — pushes toward 75%.

Suggested order to add books

Add roughly one new book per week. Give yourself time to fully convert each before claiming the next — bonus bets expire in 7-14 days.

Offer numbers on this page are examples, not quotes. Books rotate their welcome promos constantly — the dollar figures below are a snapshot of typical shapes. The order and the method are the durable parts. Always check each signup page for today’s exact offer, and run whatever number you see through the same steps.

The strategy: scale from smallest to largest. Bigger welcome offers need bigger bankrolls to play through — BetMGM's $1,500 first-bet match isn't realistic on $200 of float. Run the small-deposit bonuses first to build cash, then graduate to the bigger ones once you have the liquidity. Save the heaviest for last.

  1. Caesars + ProphetX — your starting pair (small first claim, sharp hedge book)
  2. Hard Rock Bet — $150 bonus bets if your $5 qualifier wins (example offer). Different structure than DK/FD: bet a heavy favorite to trigger the bonus, hedge the dog at a second book. Read the Hard Rock overview →
  3. Fanatics — modest payout, builds bankroll a bit more
  4. DraftKings — $100 in bonus bets (example offer: bet $5, get 4 × $25). A great starter offer for the bonus-churn bankroll. Read the DK overview →
  5. FanDuel — $150 in bonus bets (example offer: bet $5, get $150). Another great starter offer in the bonus-churn bankroll build. Read the FD overview →
  6. BetMGM — up to $1,500 first-bet match (CO). Save for last — needs real bankroll for the qualifier and the hedge. Read the BetMGM overview →
  7. Bet105 / Bookmaker — sharp hedge prices for obscure markets, no welcome bonus to chase
  8. Underdog / PrizePicks — different mechanics (pick-em DFS), separate playbook from traditional sportsbooks

If you're not in a state where some of these are legal, skip those. You can still build a solid bankroll on whichever ones are available to you.
