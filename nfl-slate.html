How to read the slate

  1. Pick the game, then the market. Passing, rushing, receiving, combos, touchdowns, kicking — the same tabs you'd walk at a props site.
  2. Line = the market's main number on that player right now, and every hit rate is graded against that number, not a round one. Best is the top price for the side you're on; Fair is the vig-free price from the two-sided books.
  3. L5 / L10 / Season are how often he's cleared it (or stayed under, if you flip the side). Last is his most recent game. The rows sort by L10, so the streaks float up.
  4. Matchup is what this week's opponent has allowed per game to his position, ranked 1–32 (1 = stingiest; green = soft). Report is the official injury designation — the questionable ones are the ones to skip.
🔒 Lock this in A hit rate is a description, not a prediction. The price is what you pay for it — a 9/10 at −160 and a 6/10 at +120 can be the same bet.
