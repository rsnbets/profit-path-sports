How hedging works: Place a bet on the opposite outcome of a wager you already have. The right hedge size depends on what you want — guaranteed profit, downside protection, or just trimming risk. The strategies above each take a different approach to balancing risk vs. upside.
How to use this

Lock in profit or limit risk

  1. Enter your original bet — the odds and stake you already have working.
  2. Enter the current opposite-side line at any sportsbook — the price you'd pay to hedge right now.
  3. Pick a strategy: equal profit (lock in the same return either way), guaranteed profit (smaller cushion, more upside if original wins), or a custom split.
  4. Read the hedge stake and the net profit for both outcomes side-by-side.
Why this version helps: Most hedge calculators give you one number for one strategy. We show multiple strategies side-by-side — equal profit, guaranteed profit, no-loss — so you can compare risk vs. upside at a glance and pick the version that matches what you actually want.

Pro tip: partial hedges leave some upside if the original wins; full hedges guarantee a fixed return. Pick based on whether you want a lock or a tilt.
