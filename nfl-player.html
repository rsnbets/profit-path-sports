How to use the NFL prop finder

  1. Search a player, pick a prop. The line stepper starts at a typical number for his position — nudge it to whatever the books hang on him.
  2. Read the clear rates. L10/L5/L3 show form; "vs" his upcoming opponent shows the head-to-head — with the sample printed on it, because two meetings is trivia, not a trend.
  3. Flip Over/Under — every panel follows the side you're actually betting.
  4. Mind the season boundary. Until 2026 weeks pile up, most of what you're reading is 2025 — same player, new defensive coordinators everywhere.
