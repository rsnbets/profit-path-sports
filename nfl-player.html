How to use the NFL prop finder

  1. Search a player, pick a prop. The line snaps to the market's main number when one is posted; tap any alt line on the strip, or step to any threshold.
  2. Read the matchup and the report. The matchup line is what this week’s opponent has allowed per game to his position, ranked against all 32 (green = soft). The badge on his name is the official injury report; questionable players are the ones JD skips.
  3. Read the clear rates. L10/L5/L3 show form; "vs" his upcoming opponent shows the head-to-head — with the sample printed on it, because two meetings is trivia, not a trend.
  4. Flip Over/Under — every panel follows the side you're actually betting.
  5. Build the combo before you take an SGP. Pick two or more of his props and the panel counts the games where all of them landed. Two props on one player move together, so the real chance is usually higher than multiplying the prices — paste your book's parlay price to see how much of that correlation it's keeping.
  6. Mind the season boundary. Until 2026 weeks pile up, most of what you're reading is 2025 — same player, new defensive coordinators everywhere.
🔒 Lock this in Stats are snapshotted daily from official league data; lines refresh four times a day on game days. History finds the spot, the price makes the bet — and the fair price tells you whether the book is charging for it.
