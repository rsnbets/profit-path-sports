How to use this

Convert bonus bets to cash

  1. Enter your bonus bet amount (e.g. $200).
  2. Enter the long-odds line you'll bet the bonus on at Book A — typically +300 or longer, since long odds maximize conversion.
  3. Enter the hedge line at Book B — the favorite at the matching odds (e.g. −300 to −400 if the bonus is on +400).
  4. Read your hedge stake and the net profit locked in regardless of which side wins.
Why this version helps: We show both the exact hedge stake and a rounded-to-the-nearest-dollar version, since real-world stakes are easier to place. We also show profit on both outcomes side-by-side so you can sanity-check the lock before placing the bets.

Pro tip: hedge on a sharp book or peer-to-peer exchange (ProphetX) for tightest pricing — and to avoid being flagged for matched-betting at recreational books.
